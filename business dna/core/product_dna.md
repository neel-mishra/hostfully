# Product DNA: TLDR

## 1. Value Proposition

### For Readers (Demand Side)
* **Core Value Prop:** Keep up with tech in 5 minutes a day. Free, daily email with curated summaries of the most important stories in startups, tech, and programming.
* **The "Jobs to be Done":**
  1.  **Stay informed:** Know what's happening in tech without spending hours on Twitter, Hacker News, Reddit, and 20 browser tabs.
  2.  **Look smart in meetings:** Walk into any conversation with context on the day's biggest stories.
  3.  **Discover tools & opportunities:** Surface relevant products, launches, and career moves from trusted curation.

### For Advertisers (Supply Side)
* **Core Value Prop:** Reach 7M+ tech professionals in a high-trust, low-noise environment that outperforms LinkedIn, Google, and Meta on cost-per-click and lead quality.
* **The "Jobs to be Done":**
  1.  **Generate qualified leads:** Drive signups, demo requests, and pipeline from senior technical decision-makers.
  2.  **Build brand awareness:** Get in front of developers, PMs, and executives who influence purchasing decisions.
  3.  **Outperform paid social:** Achieve lower CPC (50% less than LinkedIn), higher engagement, and better lead quality than saturated social platforms.

## 2. Product Moat (The "Magic")
*   **Curation Quality:** Human-curated content that filters signal from noise. Not algorithmic — editorial judgment selects only stories worth reading. This is what earns 40-48% open rates (vs. 15-20% industry average).
*   **Audience Concentration:** 7M+ tech professionals in one channel. Over 60% in technical roles, over 30% manager+ level. Majority US-based. This density is nearly impossible to replicate on paid social.
*   **Trust & Habit:** Readers choose to open TLDR every morning. Unlike social feeds where ads interrupt, TLDR ads are read alongside trusted editorial content — native, non-intrusive, and contextually relevant.
*   **Low Ad Density:** Maximum 3 sponsors per newsletter. Every ad gets attention. No banner blindness, no scroll fatigue, no competing with 50 other advertisers on the same page.

## 4. The 2-Sided Network in Product Design
*   **Reader experience shapes advertiser value:** High-quality curation → high open rates → each ad impression is more valuable → advertisers pay premium → revenue funds better curation → flywheel spins faster.
*   **Ad format protects reader experience:** Native text ads that match editorial tone. No pop-ups, no banners, no autoplay video. Maximum 3 per issue. This restraint is a product decision — it keeps readers opening every day, which is what makes the inventory valuable.
*   **Newsletter segmentation serves both sides:** 12 verticals let readers subscribe to exactly what they want AND let advertisers target specific audiences (e.g., AI engineers, DevOps professionals, startup founders) without wasted impressions.

---
### AI Agent Context Rule:
When drafting product content, always specify which side of the network you're addressing:
- **Reader content:** Lead with **time saved** and **curation quality**. The product is the newsletter itself.
- **Advertiser content:** Lead with **ROI**, **audience quality**, and **performance vs. social**. The product is the ad placement.
- **Internal/strategic content:** Frame around the **flywheel** — every decision should strengthen both sides of the network simultaneously.
