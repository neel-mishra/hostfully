# 🚀 GTM Launch Playbook: Master Orchestration

## ## 1. Phase 1: Foundation & Alignment (Weeks 1-2)
* **Goal:** Ensure the product mission matches the market reality.
* **Agent Instructions:**
    1.  Read `@core/business_context.md` and `@core/product_dna.md` to identify the core value prop.
    2.  Cross-reference `@core/ideal_customer_profile.md` to select the specific **Buyer Persona** for this launch.
    3.  Review `@core/competitor_landscape.md` to identify which competitor we are "displacing" with this feature.

## ## 2. Phase 2: Asset Creation (Weeks 3-4)
* **Goal:** Generate all internal and external documentation.
* **Agent Instructions:**
    1.  **Draft the PRD:** Use `@ops/prd_template.md` to finalize technical and functional specs.
    2.  **Define the Hook:** Use `@identity/messaging_pillars.md` to choose the primary marketing "angle."
    3.  **Draft Internal Comms:** Use `@identity/style_guide_internal.md` to brief the CEO and CTO on the launch timeline.

## ## 3. Phase 3: Multi-Channel Execution (Week 5 - Launch)
* **Goal:** Distribute the message across all platforms with brand consistency.
* **Agent Instructions:**
    1.  **Ad Copy:** Use `@identity/ad_copy_frameworks.md` to draft Google, Meta, and LinkedIn ads.
    2.  **External Announcement:** Follow the `@ops/release_announcement_workflow.md` to draft the email and blog post.
    3.  **Creative Brief:** Use `@identity/creative_direction.md` to generate prompts for visual assets.

---

## ## 4. Launch Day Rituals
* **Social Blitz:** Execute the "Founder Repost Cascade" on LinkedIn.
* **Community Drop:** Post a high-value "Problem-Solution" thread on Reddit (r/vacationrentals) and specialized FB Groups.
* **Monitoring:** Use `@ops/meeting_summarizer_protocol.md` to capture feedback from the first 5 beta-tester calls.

## ## 5. Post-Launch: The Feedback Loop
* **Success Check:** Compare results against the **KPIs** defined in the specific PRD.
* **Intelligence Update:** Update `@ops/customer_summary_intelligence.md` with real "Voice of Customer" quotes from the launch.
* **Strategy Refresh:** If the data shows a pivot is needed, update the **Messaging Pillars** or **ICP** files.
