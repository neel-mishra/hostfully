# Ad Copy Frameworks: Hostfully (Hospitality / STR Software)

Goal: Help GTM teams draft **credible**, **segment-aware** copy for **operators** evaluating property management and guest-experience software. Anchor to [messaging_pillars.md](messaging_pillars.md) and quantitative context in [ideal_customer_profile.md](../core/ideal_customer_profile.md).

**Not in scope:** Newsletter subscriber acquisition, ad-network inventory sales, or generic B2B SaaS without hospitality specificity.

---

## 1. Demand Gen — Operators (Primary)

### Search (Google)
*   **Intent:** Operators actively comparing PMS options, direct booking, inbox, automation—often by **problem** ("double booking," "guest messaging," "channel manager").
*   **Headline patterns:** `[Outcome] for [STR / vacation rentals]` · `[Problem] → [Hostfully capability]` · `[Portfolio scale] property managers`
*   **Description patterns:** Name **unified inbox**, **automation**, **direct booking**, **guidebooks**—only where product truth supports it.
*   **Keywords (examples):** vacation rental software, property management software, short-term rental PMS, channel manager alternative (use carefully—category accuracy matters).

### Paid Social (Meta, LinkedIn)
*   **Strategy:** Creative-first hooks tied to **daily pain**—inbox overload, OTA dependence, team coordination.
*   **Visual:** See [creative_direction.md](creative_direction.md)—operators, real work contexts, not generic "hype office."
*   **CTA:** Demo, talk to sales, or resource—match **tier** (SMB self-serve vs mid-market conversation).

### Remarketing
*   **Strategy:** Reinforce **one** proof per ad—implementation, integration, or segment-appropriate outcome.
*   **Avoid:** Fake urgency, competitor trash talk (see [competitor_landscape.md](../core/competitor_landscape.md)).

---

## 2. Lifecycle & Expansion

### Customer / lifecycle email
*   **Goal:** Adoption of **next** capability (guidebooks, automation, direct booking surfaces)—tie to **their** segment and property count.
*   **Tone:** Helpful partner from [brand_voice_matrix.md](brand_voice_matrix.md)—never guilt or spam frequency lies.

### Partner / integration
*   **Goal:** Clarify **technical fit** and **shared customer** value—API, ecosystem, co-marketing.

---

## 3. Compliance & Honesty

*   **Numbers:** When citing MRR, CLV, retention, or channel performance—cite **export month/year** from ICP (e.g. Feb 2026 segmentation, Jan 2026 CLV).
*   **No guaranteed ROI:** Case patterns and benchmarks are fine; **no** promises of specific revenue lift for a prospect without a signed scope.
*   **Competitors:** Respectful comparison—fit and tradeoffs, not smear.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule
When generating ad copy: **1)** Pick **pillar(s)** from [messaging_pillars.md](messaging_pillars.md). **2)** Pick **portfolio tier** and **region** from ICP. **3)** State **product scope** (PMP vs PMP + Guidebooks) if the feature is relevant. **4)** Never use **legacy newsletter/advertiser** frameworks from old drafts.
