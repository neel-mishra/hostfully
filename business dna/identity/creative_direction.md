# Creative & Visual Direction: TLDR

## 1. Visual Identity & Palette
*   **Primary Colors:**
    *   **TLDR Blue:** `#2563EB` (Trust, clarity, tech-forward)
    *   **TLDR Black:** `#0F172A` (Sleek, modern, high-contrast)
*   **Secondary & Accent Colors:**
    *   **Signal Green:** `#10B981` (Growth, subscribers, "open rate up")
    *   **Warm Amber:** `#F59E0B` (Attention, urgency, CTAs, featured content)
    *   **Soft Gray:** `#F1F5F9` (Light backgrounds, clean layouts)
    *   **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Newsletter body, clean readability)
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Headlines:** Bold, clean sans-serif (Inter, system fonts). High legibility at all sizes.
    *   **Body:** Readable, comfortable at 16px+. Newsletter content must feel effortless to scan.
    *   **Code/Technical:** Monospace (JetBrains Mono, SF Mono) when showing technical content.

## 2. Imagery Style (The "Look")

### Reader-Facing Creative
*   **Tone & Mood:** Clean, minimal, informational. "The smart morning routine."
*   **Visual Metaphors:**
    *   **The Inbox:** A clean, organized inbox with one standout email — TLDR.
    *   **The Clock:** 5 minutes visualized — a timer, a coffee pour, a commute segment.
    *   **The Curated Feed:** Contrast chaotic social feeds with TLDR's clean, curated format.
*   **Human Elements:** Professionals in their element — reading on a phone during commute, scanning before a meeting. Relaxed confidence, not stressed hustle.
*   **Color Treatment:** Bright, high-contrast, blue-dominant. White backgrounds for readability. Green accents for stats and social proof.

### Advertiser-Facing Creative
*   **Tone & Mood:** Professional, data-driven, business-to-business. "The performance channel."
*   **Visual Metaphors:**
    *   **The Dashboard:** ROI metrics, pipeline numbers, CPC comparisons visualized as clean charts.
    *   **The Comparison:** Side-by-side TLDR vs. LinkedIn/Google/Meta performance.
    *   **The Case Study Card:** Headline stat + company logo + quote pull.
*   **Human Elements:** Marketing leaders in strategic settings — whiteboard sessions, analytics reviews. Confident, results-oriented.
*   **Color Treatment:** Darker, more corporate. TLDR Black backgrounds with blue and green data visualizations. White text on dark for impact.

## 3. Ad Creative Guidelines

### For Reader Acquisition Ads (LinkedIn, Meta, Google)
*   **Formats that work:** Static images (1:1 for LinkedIn/Meta), short-form video (15-30s), carousel (multi-benefit).
*   **Visual formula:** Bold stat or hook text + clean background + TLDR logo + CTA.
*   **Always include:** Subscriber count ("1,600,000+ readers"), time value ("5 minutes"), and "Free" prominently.
*   **Never include:** Advertising pricing, CPM/CPC metrics, or advertiser testimonials. These are for the other side.

### For Advertiser Acquisition Ads (LinkedIn, Google)
*   **Formats that work:** Static images with data/stats, case study cards, comparison charts.
*   **Visual formula:** Headline ROI stat + advertiser logo + "See the case study" CTA.
*   **Always include:** Specific numbers (52x ROI, $1M pipeline, 50% lower CPC), advertiser social proof.
*   **Never include:** Subscriber count framing ("join readers"), newsletter content previews, or "free" messaging.

## 4. Brand Symbols & Motifs
*   **The TLDR Logo:** Clean wordmark. Primary: blue on white. Inverted: white on dark. Always legible.
*   **The Envelope / Inbox:** Recurring motif for reader-facing content. The daily email, delivered.
*   **The Bar Chart / Arrow Up:** Recurring motif for advertiser-facing content. Performance, growth, ROI.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule:
When requesting visuals, always specify which audience the creative targets:
*   **Reader acquisition:** Bright, clean, blue-dominant. Lead with subscriber count, time savings, "free."
*   **Advertiser acquisition:** Dark, data-rich, professional. Lead with ROI stats, CPC comparisons, case study proof.
*   **Never mix visual languages** between the two audiences. A reader ad should never look like a media kit. An advertiser ad should never look like a newsletter signup page.
