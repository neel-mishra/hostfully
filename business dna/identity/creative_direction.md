# Creative & Visual Direction: Hostfully

## 1. Visual Identity & Palette
*   **Primary Colors:**
    *   **Hostfully Blue:** `#2563EB` (Trust, clarity, tech-forward)
    *   **Hostfully Black:** `#0F172A` (Sleek, modern, high-contrast)
*   **Secondary & Accent Colors:**
    *   **Signal Green:** `#10B981` (Growth, retention, positive movement)
    *   **Warm Amber:** `#F59E0B` (Attention, urgency, CTAs, featured content)
    *   **Soft Gray:** `#F1F5F9` (Light backgrounds, clean layouts)
    *   **White:** `#FFFFFF` (UI, docs, readability)
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Headlines:** Bold, clean sans-serif (Inter, system fonts). High legibility at all sizes.
    *   **Body:** Readable, comfortable at 16px+. Product and marketing pages must scan quickly.
    *   **Code/Technical:** Monospace when showing API, integrations, or technical setup content.

## 2. Imagery Style (The "Look")

### Operator-Facing Creative (Primary GTM)
*   **Tone & Mood:** Calm, competent, hospitality-real. "Run the day without chaos."
*   **Visual Metaphors:**
    *   **Unified inbox / calendar:** One pane—messages, bookings, tasks—vs scattered tabs.
    *   **The property portfolio:** Scalable row of homes → growth without losing control (match **tier A–E** subtly, not stock clichés).
    *   **Guest journey:** Arrival, guidebook on phone, smooth check-in—human warmth + software clarity.
*   **Human Elements:** Property managers and hosts in real work settings—desk, field, team huddle—not generic "stock startup."
*   **Color Treatment:** Bright, high-contrast, blue-dominant; white backgrounds; green for positive metrics (occupancy, direct bookings, time saved).

### Enterprise / Partner-Facing Creative
*   **Tone & Mood:** Professional, architectural, integration-forward. "Built for serious operations."
*   **Visual Metaphors:**
    *   **Stack diagram:** Hostfully connected to OTAs, locks, payments, BI.
    *   **Governance:** Roles, permissions, audit-friendly workflows.
    *   **API / developer:** Clean code blocks, endpoint lists—sparingly, for the right audience.
*   **Human Elements:** Ops leaders, revenue owners, implementation—confident, not flashy.
*   **Color Treatment:** May use darker panels (Hostfully Black) with blue/green data accents for **contrast** with SMB creative—still the same brand, not a different company.

## 3. Ad Creative Guidelines

### Demand Gen (Operators: LinkedIn, Meta, Google)
*   **Formats:** Static 1:1, short video (15–45s), carousel for multi-benefit (inbox, automation, direct booking).
*   **Visual formula:** Outcome headline + product truth + single CTA (demo / talk to sales / start trial—whatever is live).
*   **Include when true:** Portfolio-tier appropriate proof (time saved, fewer errors, direct booking)—see [ideal_customer_profile.md](../core/ideal_customer_profile.md).
*   **Avoid:** Fake subscriber counts, newsletter framing, or ad-network CPC claims—wrong business model.

### Lifecycle & Product (Email, in-app)
*   **Formats:** Simple layouts, one goal per message; in-app modals consistent with product UI.
*   **Visual formula:** Problem → specific Hostfully capability → next step.
*   **Never:** Dense spreadsheets as hero art; PII from customers in screenshots.

## 4. Brand Symbols & Motifs
*   **The Hostfully Logo:** Clean wordmark. Primary: blue on white. Inverted: white on dark.
*   **Connected operations:** Calendar + message bubble + home icon (abstract)—signals **one system** without clutter.
*   **Growth without chaos:** Measured upward trend—not lottery-winner hype.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule
When requesting visuals, specify:
*   **Audience:** Operator SMB (**A–B**) vs mid-market (**C–D**) vs large (**E**) vs partner/enterprise.
*   **Proof type:** Operational (time/errors), revenue (direct bookings), guest (guidebooks/communication).
*   **Do not** use legacy **reader vs advertiser** creative splits; Hostfully is **hospitality software**, not a media network.
