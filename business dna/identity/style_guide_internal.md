# Internal Communication Style: Hostfully Team

## 1. The Core Philosophy
*   **Standard:** Async-first, concise, transparent. We build software that saves operators time—internal communication should model the same discipline.
*   **Philosophy:** "Respect people's attention." If you can say it in one sentence, don't write three. If it's documented, link to it.
*   **Execution:** Clear writing = clear thinking. Internal docs should be scannable in under two minutes.

---

## 2. Tone & Diction
*   **Personality:** **Clear Operator Partner.** Direct, practical, evidence-seeking—like a great CS or solutions conversation.
*   **Technicality:** Medium-high. Product, GTM, and CS should share fluency in **MRR/ARR**, **cohort retention**, **portfolio tiers (A–E)**, **PMP vs Guidebooks**, **integrations**, and **implementation** milestones—not media metrics.
*   **Transparency:** Open by default. Pipeline, churn, NRR, and customer feedback deserve crisp circulation. Bad news early is fixable; surprises late are expensive.

---

## 3. The "This, Not That" Internal Framework
| We Are... | But Never... | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Customer-grounded** | Theatrical | Operators live in bookings, guests, and owners—speak that language. |
| **Data-informed** | Paralyzed by data | Metrics guide decisions; they don't replace judgment. Ship, measure, iterate. |
| **Lean & fast** | Sloppy | Small teams win by focus—speed with quality bar. |
| **Product-first** | Channel-noise | Revenue follows durable product value—not the other way around. |

---

## 4. Structured Messaging Templates

### The "Metric Alert" (Retention / Product)
*   **What changed:** "Logo retention at M4 for cohort X moved ±N pts—here's the cohort definition."
*   **Hypothesis:** "Onboarding step Y correlates with early churn in segment A–B."
*   **Next step:** "CS experiment: guided setup for inbox connect in first 7 days."

### The "Customer Signal" (Sales / CS)
*   **Account:** "[Segment tier] — [use case]."
*   **Signal:** "Upgrade motion after guidebooks attach" or "Downgrade risk after listing drop."
*   **Follow-up:** "Playbook: expansion vs save."

### The "GTM Update" (Acquisition)
*   **Channel:** "Website chat vs referral vs demo—per last 12-month rollup."
*   **Numbers:** Signups and opportunity MRR from internal export—label **month/year** of file.
*   **Insight:** "Message-market fit for mid-market may need separate creative from SMB."

---

## 5. Communication Rituals
*   **Standups:** Async. Blockers, wins, customer signal—minimal bullets.
*   **Weekly business review:** Revenue, funnel, churn, NRR, CS load—numbers first, narrative second.
*   **Customer escalations:** Facts, impact, owner, next step—no blame framing.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule
Internal communications should be **brief, data-backed, and actionable**. Assume competence. Link to **source exports** (e.g. [ideal_customer_profile.md](../core/ideal_customer_profile.md)) instead of pasting full tables.
