# Style Guides: Hostfully

## 1. Operator-Facing Editorial (Default)

*   **Audience:** Property managers, hosts, hospitality operators—often **time-poor** and **skeptical of generic SaaS hype**.
*   **Goal:** Trust and clarity. Readers should recognize Hostfully as **practical**, **direct**, and **grounded in hospitality**.

### Voice
| Principle | Do | Don't |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Concrete** | Name workflows: inbox, calendar, channels, guests, owners | Vague "synergy" or "platformize" |
| **Honest** | Acknowledge tradeoffs and fit | Overclaim "best" without proof |
| **Warm-professional** | Sound like a strong partner | Stiff enterprise jargon or cutesy tone |

### Key phrases (examples—not mandatory)
* "Property management," "short-term rental," "direct bookings," "guest experience," "one connected system"
* "Automate," "simplify," "scale," "operations"

---

## 2. Growth & Sales (Pipeline)

*   **Audience:** Buyers comparing **alternatives**; may include revenue owners and ops leaders.
*   **Goal:** Clear **next step** (demo, trial, business review) with **credible** proof.

### Practices
* Lead with **outcome** for their **tier** (small portfolio vs multi-market).
* Use **numbers** only when sourced from internal exports—see [ideal_customer_profile.md](../core/ideal_customer_profile.md).
* Competitor comparisons: **respectful** and **use-case** based ([competitor_landscape.md](../core/competitor_landscape.md)).

---

## 3. Product & Technical

*   **Audience:** Implementers, admins, integration partners.
*   **Goal:** Precision—**API**, **integrations**, **permissions**, **setup steps**—without drowning in jargon.

---

## 4. Translation Layer (Quick Reference)

| Context | Emphasis |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Website / blog** | Operator editorial voice (Section 1) |
| **Paid / lifecycle** | Growth voice (Section 2) with **pillar** alignment |
| **Docs / API** | Technical voice (Section 3) |

**Do not mix** hospitality-operator copy with **newsletter** or **ad-network** metaphors—those are obsolete frames for Hostfully.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule
Choose **one** primary style per asset. When unsure, default to **Operator-Facing Editorial** (Section 1) and pull proof from [business_context.md](../core/business_context.md) and [ideal_customer_profile.md](../core/ideal_customer_profile.md).
