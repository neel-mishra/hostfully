# Numeral Distribution GTM Deck Outline

*Target Audience: Executive Leadership*
*Objective: Outline strategy, targets, and expected outcomes for entering the Distribution market.*

## Slide 1: Executive Summary & The Paradigm Shift
*   **The Distribution Opportunity**: Moving from B2C retail efficiency (the "time-savings" play) to wholesale B2B risk mitigation.
*   **The Core Hypothesis**: Wholesale distributors carry massive compliance liability, specifically in managing Exemption Certificates across multiple states. 
*   **The Strategy**: By gating features like Exemption Certificate Management behind our **Pro (Custom)** tier, we can force this segment into a value-based, sales-led motion, drastically increasing our ACV.

## Slide 2: Target Audience & Identification
**Who should we target? (The Buying Committee)**
*   Wholesale Distributors ($50M+ ARR) with substantial geographic footprints and complex supply chains.
*   **The Champion / Pain Holder**: Director of Tax & Compliance (Feels the daily pain of tracking Exemption Certificates and manual ERP data entry).
*   **The Economic Buyer / Risk Bearer**: CFO / VP of Finance (Holds the budget and holds the legal/financial liability of a failed state audit).
*   **The Technical Evaluator**: IT Director / ERP Administrator (Ensures Numeral's API can securely integrate with their legacy NetSuite/Acumatica systems).

**How do we target them? (The Data Execution Flow)**
*When visualizing this slide, present it as a 3-step funnel/flowchart:*
1.  **Technographic Profiling (The Base)**: 
    *   Querying data providers (BuiltWith, Datanyze) to identify companies running specific B2B ERPs (NetSuite, Acumatica, Sage Intacct).
2.  **Firmographic Filtering (The Fit)**: 
    *   Enriching the technographic list (via Apollo/Clearbit) to filter for NAICS codes (Wholesale/Distribution), employee headcount, and revenue thresholds ($50M+).
3.  **Job Title Filtering & Persona Mapping (The Target)**:
    *   Scraping LinkedIn/Apollo within the filtered accounts for exact Buyer Personas (e.g., "VP Finance", "Tax Director").
    *   Mapping specific "Risk-Based" messaging to the CFO layer, and "Operational Efficiency" messaging to the Tax Director layer.

## Slide 3: Website & Digital Experience Alignment
**Is there anything that we need to do with our website?**
Yes, introduce strategic friction to drive high-value conversations while accelerating trust.
*   **Building an Integrations Page**: Create a dedicated, SEO-optimized directory showcasing native integrations with primary B2B ERPs (like NetSuite, Acumatica, and Sage) to drive high-intent technical search traffic and immediately address the Technical Evaluator's core needs.
*   **Messaging Pivot**: Update the Distribution/Wholesale solutions page to focus entirely on financial risk, audit preparation, and liability protection—abandoning the "time-savings" retail message.
*   **Interactive Product Demo (Reducing Time-to-Aha)**: Embed an interactive, ungated demo (e.g., Navattic/Tourial) showing the exact workflow of managing complex exemption certificates across 50 states to immediately validate capabilities before the sales call.
*   **Build Growth Interactive Tools for segmentation, lead capture, and lead nurture**:
    *   **1. The "Audit Exposure" Liability Calculator**: Wholesalers often underestimate the cost of a missing certificate. This tool quantifies the "hidden" risk.
        *   *User Input*: Annual exempt sales volume, percentage of customers with missing/expired certificates (estimate slider), and states of operation.
        *   *Output*: A projected dollar amount of "Uncollated Tax Liability" if audited today.
        *   *Growth Hook*: "Your current documentation gap could cost you $240,000 in a 3-year audit lookback. Download the 'Wholesale Audit Defense Checklist' to see how to close it."
    *   **2. Multi-State Nexus "Blindspot" Detector**: Wholesalers often trigger nexus through inventory in 3PL warehouses (like Amazon FBA or ShipBob) or having independent sales reps in other states—factors they often overlook.
        *   *User Input*: A checklist of activities (e.g., "Do you use a 3PL?", "Do you attend trade shows?", "Do you have remote sales staff?").
        *   *Output*: A heat map or list of states where they likely have nexus but aren't registered.
        *   *Growth Hook*: This is a high-value tool because it solves a "scary" unknown. The lead capture occurs before revealing the specific states.
    *   **3. Exemption Certificate "Rules Engine" (State-by-State)**: Wholesale complexity often stems from whether a state accepts an out-of-state certificate or requires their specific state form (e.g., California and Florida are notoriously strict).
        *   *User Input*: "I am located in [State A]" and "I am shipping to a customer in [State B]."
        *   *Output*: A dynamic result showing which form is required (SST, MTC, or State-specific) and if a home-state tax ID is sufficient.
        *   *Growth Hook*: "Tired of looking up these rules? Enter your email to get our 'Master Matrix of State Exemption Requirements' updated for 2024."
    *   **4. Drop-Ship "Tax Pyramid" Simulator**: Drop-shipping is a tax nightmare for wholesalers. If a wholesaler drop-ships for a retailer into a state where the retailer isn't registered, the wholesaler may be forced to charge sales tax to the retailer—eating into margins.
        *   *User Input*: Wholesaler Nexus States, Retailer Nexus States, and Shipping Destination.
        *   *Output*: A flow chart showing who is responsible for the certificate and whether tax must be collected.
        *   *Growth Hook*: This targets the "messiest" part of wholesale. The tool can end with: "Our API automates these complex three-party logic flows. Book a 15-minute workflow audit."
    *   **5. Use Tax "Leakage" Estimator for Manufacturers/Distributors**: Wholesalers often pull items from "resale" inventory to use as samples, for marketing, or for internal repairs. This triggers "Use Tax."
        *   *User Input*: Monthly value of "inventory withdrawn for internal use" and primary business location.
        *   *Output*: Monthly and annual Use Tax obligation.
        *   *Growth Hook*: Most wholesalers ignore this until an auditor finds it. "You could be under-paying Use Tax by $X/year. Get our 'Inventory-to-Use Tax' automation guide."
    *   **Why these work for Numeral**:
        *   **Address Specificity**: Generic tax calculators are for consumers. These tools speak the language of "Resale Certificates" and "Nexus," which signals to a Wholesale VP of Finance that Numeral understands their specific pain.
        *   **High Intent**: Anyone using a "Drop-Ship Tax Logic" tool is feeling an immediate operational pain point, making them a high-quality lead for a sales conversation.
        *   **SEO Value**: These tools rank for long-tail keywords like "Does Florida accept out of state resale certificates?" which are the exact queries your target personas are googling.
*   **Integration Ecosystem Visibility**: Create a dedicated, highly-visible banner or section showing native integrations with the primary B2B ERPs (NetSuite, Acumatica, Sage) to immediately answer the Technical Evaluator's primary objection.
*   **CTA Strategy**: Remove transactional/self-serve pricing for this ICP. Use a strong "Contact Sales" CTA to force discussions on value-based pricing.

## Slide 4: Marketing Channels & Go-To-Market Mechanics
**Which marketing channels could be most effective?**

*   **Prioritization**: Account-Based Marketing (ABM) and highly targeted B2B Demand Generation over broad awareness.
*   **How do you think about the channel mix?**
    1.  **AI-Driven Outbound (High Priority)**: Using Clay to orchestrate hyper-personalized email sequences targeting CFOs/Finance leaders regarding specific audit risks.
    2.  **Paid Ads & Social (High Priority)**: High-value content (e.g., "The CFO's Exemption Guide") targeted at B2B titles. **Crucially, deploying a "Clay Ads Strategy"**: Using Clay to build out static, highly targeted audiences of our Named Accounts to upload directly into LinkedIn/Meta for zero waste ad spend.
    3.  **SEO & Bottom-of-Funnel Content (Medium Priority)**: Creating deep-dive programmatic content targeting queries like "Wholesale Exemption Certificate Management Software."
    4.  **Partner & Integrator Alliances (Medium Priority)**: Co-marketing and strategic referrals via B2B ERP system integrators (e.g., NetSuite/Acumatica consultants).
    5.  **Targeted Digital PR (Low Priority)**: Thought leadership placed in specific trade publications focused on Supply Chain Finance and B2B Distribution.
    6.  **Offline/Event Marketing (Low Priority)**: For later stages; sponsoring or attending high-level Finance Executive summits within the distribution vertical.
*   **How do you think about creatives?**
    *   **The "Risk/Fear Play"**: Visualizing the financial devastation and chaos of a failed multi-state tax audit.
    *   **The "Trust/Security Play"**: Emphasizing complete coverage, unified reporting, and operational peace of mind.
*   **How do you think about allocating budget in the early days?**
    *   **50%** towards outbound infrastructure and data curation (Apollo/Intent tools/Clay) to ensure hyper-targeted list building and the Clay Ads Strategy.
    *   **30%** towards high-intent Search Ads and LinkedIn lead gen/retargeting for those key CFO personas.
    *   **20%** reserved for **Contract Buyout Subsidies**: A dedicated budget to buy target accounts out of their existing legacy contracts (Avalara/Vertex) to accelerate the sales cycle.

## Slide 5: Path to Scale
**How do you think about scaling?**
*   **Intent-Driven Triggers**: Move from manual outbound to automated ABM triggers (e.g., outreach immediately launched when a distributor hits a new state nexus or expands a warehouse).
*   **Integration Ecosystem**: Build deeper native integrations with legacy B2B ERPs to reduce onboarding friction and create a structural moat.
*   **Pricing Expansion**: Introduce volume-based pricing tiers within the Custom Pro package based on the sheer number of exemption certificates managed.

## Slide 6: Risk Mitigation & Challenges
**What could go wrong?**
*   **Integration Bottlenecks (The Implementation Risk)**: Legacy distributors often run heavily customized, on-premise ERPs with messy data architectures. 
    *   *Mitigation*: Develop standardized middleware playbooks and partner with specialized system integrators (SIs) to offset the burden on our internal engineering teams.
*   **Incumbent Entrenchment**: Established players (Avalara/Vertex) are deeply embedded in these organizations, making "rip-and-replace" proposals highly resistant to budget approval.
    *   *Mitigation*: Sell the "Exemption Certificate Module" as an agile wedge. **Additionally, offer aggressive Contract Buyouts** to eliminate the financial friction of switching before their legacy renewal date.
*   **Long Sales Cycles**: Enterprise sales involve intense legal, security, and procurement reviews, potentially freezing pipeline velocity.
    *   *Mitigation*: Co-opt the Technical Evaluator early with robust security documentation and sandbox API access during the presales motion.
*   **Market Disconnect**: Gracefully disqualifying smaller wholesalers who demand Exemption Certificates but fall below our Custom ACV threshold without damaging brand sentiment.
    *   *Mitigation*: Implement clear routing rules on the website to filter these leads into a generalized partner network or an automated self-serve rejection nurture.

## Slide 7: Sales & Marketing Symbiosis
**To what extent would you partner with our sales org? (e.g., our SDR team)**
Marketing's primary mandate in this segment is Pipeline Acceleration and SDR Enablement.
*   **Targeted Alignment & Outbound Enrichment**: Marketing and Sales jointly agree on a strict Named Account List (ABM). Marketing runs the "Clay Engine" to enrich these accounts with deep technographic signals, ensuring SDRs are only calling into highly prioritized, data-rich accounts.
*   **Advanced Lead Segmentation & Nurture Routing**: 
    *   Implement an intelligent routing system where high-intent, high-fit enterprise leads (e.g., $100M+ ARR seeking Exemption Certificates) bypass standard SDR qualification and route directly to Senior Account Executives.
    *   Medium/low-intent MQLs enter specialized long-term nurture tracks focused on multi-state audit risks before SDR intervention.
*   **The Handoff Playbook (MQL \> SQL)**: Establishing a strict SLA for when an account transitions from Marketing Nurture to SDR Outreach (e.g., triggered by a specific pricing page visit or a high-score interaction with the interactive demo).
*   **White-Glove Onboarding (Post-Sale Handoff)**: Marketing designs the post-signature experience in tandem with Customer Success. For Distribution enterprise accounts, we replace self-serve onboarding with a "White-Glove Implementation Plan" (including dedicated solution architects and timeline guarantees) to accelerate time-to-value and prevent early churn.
*   **Sales Enablement Assets**: Marketing will equip the revenue team with resources purpose-built for the Distribution segment:
    *   *Competitor Battlecards*: Objection handling for legacy ERP compliance modules vs. Numeral's modern API approach.
    *   *Positioning & Messaging Guides*: Transitioning the SDR talk track from "retail time-savings" to "wholesale audit risk mitigation."
    *   *Product Portfolio Mix & Promotions*: Clear guidelines on how and when to leverage the Pro (Custom) tier features (like Exemption Certificates) to anchor higher ACVs during negotiations.
*   **Continuous Feedback Loop**: Implementing a bi-weekly "Pipeline Council" between Marketing and Sales leadership to review closed-lost reasons, identify messaging friction, and feed SDR frontline intelligence back into the top-of-funnel ad creatives.
