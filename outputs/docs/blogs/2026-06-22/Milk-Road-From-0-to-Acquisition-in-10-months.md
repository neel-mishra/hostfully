# Milk Road: From 0 to Acquisition in 10 Months

Milk Road launched a crypto newsletter in early 2022. Ten months later, they sold it. The deal reportedly valued the company at eight figures.

That's not a normal timeline. Most media companies take years to reach acquisition-level scale. Milk Road compressed the entire journey — audience building, monetization, and exit — into less than a year. Here's how they did it, and what newsletter operators can learn from it.

## The Thesis

Milk Road's founders — Shaan Puri and Ben Levy — identified a gap: crypto was complex, fast-moving, and drowning in noise. The existing information sources were either too technical for mainstream readers or too shallow to be useful.

Their bet: a daily email that explained crypto in plain language, in five minutes or less. Not another crypto blog. Not a trading signals service. A curated briefing for people who wanted to stay informed without spending an hour on Crypto Twitter.

The format was newsletter-first from day one. No app, no website-heavy strategy. Just email — the channel with the highest engagement rates and the lowest distribution cost.

## How They Grew

### Referral programs as a growth engine

Milk Road's referral program was central to their growth. Subscribers earned rewards — exclusive content, merch, access — for every new reader they brought in. The program was fully automated, which meant the growth engine ran without manual intervention.

This mirrors what works across the newsletter space. TLDR uses a similar referral model across its 12 newsletters. The math is simple: every engaged subscriber who refers one person doubles your growth rate at zero acquisition cost.

### Cross-promotion with adjacent newsletters

Instead of competing with every crypto newsletter, Milk Road partnered with complementary ones. Cross-promotions — "we recommend you, you recommend us" — gave both sides access to warm, relevant audiences.

This is the most underrated growth lever in newsletters. The audience already trusts the recommender. Trust transfers. TLDR's newsletter swap program drives measurable subscriber growth for the same reason.

### Paid acquisition with tight feedback loops

Milk Road invested in paid social ads, primarily on platforms where crypto-curious professionals spent time. What made their approach work wasn't the budget — it was the feedback loop.

They tracked every channel by downstream metrics: open rates, retention, and unsubscribe rates — not just cost per acquisition. A cheap subscriber who never opens the email is worthless. An expensive one who reads every issue and refers friends is a bargain. They reallocated spend based on quality signals, not volume.

### Content that earned the open

The newsletter itself was the retention strategy. Every issue was concise, opinionated, and written in a voice that felt like a smart friend explaining the news over coffee. No filler, no padding, no "comprehensive overview of the blockchain landscape."

They shipped daily. Consistency built the habit. The habit built the open rates. Open rates above 40% made them irresistible to advertisers.

## The Acquisition

Within 10 months, Milk Road had built a subscriber base large enough to attract serious buyers. The high engagement metrics — open rates consistently above 40%, strong advertiser demand — made the financial case straightforward.

The acquisition wasn't luck. It was the predictable outcome of focused execution: grow fast with referrals and partnerships, retain subscribers with genuinely good content, monetize through advertising, and demonstrate that the machine works.

Newsletter acquisitions follow a clear pattern: audience quality matters more than audience size. A 250,000-subscriber list with 45% open rates is worth more than a million-subscriber list at 12%. Milk Road understood this from the start.

## What Newsletter Operators Can Learn

**Pick a niche and own it.** Milk Road didn't try to cover all of tech or all of finance. They picked crypto, went deep, and became the default daily briefing for that audience.

**Ship daily.** A daily newsletter builds a daily habit. Weekly newsletters compete with everything else in a subscriber's week. Daily ones become part of the morning routine.

**Automate your growth loops.** Referral programs that run without manual effort compound over time. Every subscriber becomes a potential growth channel.

**Track quality, not just volume.** The metric that matters isn't subscribers gained — it's subscribers retained. Open rates, not list size, determine your value to advertisers and acquirers.

**Move fast.** Milk Road launched, scaled, monetized, and exited in 10 months. Speed wasn't reckless — it was strategic. They didn't wait for perfection. They shipped, measured, and iterated daily.

The Milk Road story isn't about crypto. It's about what happens when a focused team builds the right product for the right audience and executes without hesitation.
