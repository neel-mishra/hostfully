# Video Script: Why Your Emails Land in Gmail's Promotions Tab

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Split screen — left shows an email landing in "Promotions" tab with a red X overlay, right shows the same email in "Primary" with a green check. Text overlay: "This isn't what you think."
- **Audio**: "Your emails are landing in Gmail's Promotions tab. And that's probably fine."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Misconception (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Screen recording showing Gmail inbox with Promotions tab highlighted. Animated arrows showing email flow. Text callout: "Promotions ≠ Spam"
- **Audio**: "Most email marketers treat Promotions like a death sentence. It's not. Gmail delivered your email successfully — it just filed it. Promotions is a filing cabinet, not a graveyard."

### Scene 2: Why Newsletters Always Land There (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Animated checklist appearing one by one — "Bulk sends ✓", "Scheduled delivery ✓", "HTML templates ✓", "No prior replies ✓". Each item gets a checkmark.
- **Audio**: "Newsletters hit every Promotions trigger. Bulk sends, scheduled delivery, consistent templates. Gmail classifies them as promotional because — they are. Every major newsletter lands here."

### Scene 3: The Real Fix (25-40s)
- **Visual**: Data visualization showing TLDR's 48% open rate bar next to industry average 15-20%. Text overlay: "48% open rate — from the Promotions tab."
- **Audio**: "TLDR runs 48% open rates. Industry average is 15 to 20. And we land in Promotions for most subscribers. The difference isn't tab placement. It's whether people care enough to open. Ask subscribers to move you to Primary. Encourage replies. Clean your list. Send content worth reading."

### Scene 4: The Bottom Line (40-50s)
- **Visual**: Clean text on screen: "Focus on the email, not the tab." with TLDR logo fade-in.
- **Audio**: "The newsletters with the highest open rates don't fight tab placement. They obsess over being worth reading. Focus on the email, not the tab."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual**: Blog post title card with "Read the full breakdown" and TLDR logo. Animated subscribe button.
- **Audio**: "Full tactical breakdown on the TLDR blog. Link in bio. Subscribe to TLDR — 5 minutes, free, and yes, it's probably in your Promotions tab."
