# Video Script: Why Your Emails Are Going to Gmail's Promotions Tab

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Bold text on screen: "Gmail Promotions isn't broken." Zoom into a Gmail inbox showing the Promotions tab.
- **Audio**: "Your newsletter is landing in Gmail's Promotions tab. And no — it's not because you did something wrong."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Truth About Promotions
- **Visual**: Split screen — Gmail inbox on left, a confused newsletter creator on right. Animated arrows showing how emails get sorted.
- **Audio**: "Promotions is a classification, not a penalty. Gmail sorts your email as commercial because it IS commercial — you're sending to thousands of people through bulk infrastructure."

### Scene 2: The Per-User Secret
- **Visual**: Two phone screens side by side. Same email appears in Primary on one, Promotions on the other. Text overlay: "Per-user classification."
- **Audio**: "Here's what most people don't realize: Gmail classifies per USER, not per sender. Two subscribers on the same list see the same email in different tabs."

### Scene 3: The Proof
- **Visual**: TLDR logo with stats animated: "7M+ subscribers" → "40-48% open rates" → "Promotions tab." Each stat punches in with a satisfying sound effect.
- **Audio**: "TLDR sends to seven million subscribers and gets 40 to 48 percent open rates. From the Promotions tab."

### Scene 4: What Actually Works
- **Visual**: Checklist appearing one by one with checkmarks. Clean, minimal design.
- **Audio**: "Send from a real person's name. Ask readers to drag you to Primary during onboarding. Encourage replies. And clean your list — unengaged subscribers tank your reputation."

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Fix the content. The tab takes care of itself." TLDR blog link below.
- **Audio**: "Stop trying to trick Gmail. Fix the content. The tab takes care of itself. Full breakdown on the TLDR blog."
