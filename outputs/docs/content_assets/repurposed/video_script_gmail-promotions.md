# Video Script: Why Your Emails Land in Gmail's Promotions Tab

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Close-up of phone screen showing Gmail Promotions tab with unread newsletter emails. Red "Promotions" label highlighted.
- **Audio**: "Your newsletter is landing in Gmail's Promotions tab. Stop panicking."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Misconception
- **Visual**: Split screen — left side shows "Promotions" tab, right side shows "Spam" folder with red X overlay
- **Audio**: "Promotions is NOT spam. Your email is delivered, visible, and browsable. Spam means hidden and reputation damage. Completely different."

### Scene 2: Why It Happens
- **Visual**: Animated checklist appearing on screen: "Bulk sending ✓, Scheduled cadence ✓, Commercial patterns ✓"
- **Audio**: "Gmail sorts every newsletter, every marketing email, every scheduled send into Promotions. It's pattern recognition, not a penalty."

### Scene 3: The Data
- **Visual**: Bar chart animation — "Industry average: 15-20%" vs "TLDR: 48%" with Promotions tab icon
- **Audio**: "TLDR sends to millions of subscribers. Most land in Promotions. Our open rate? 48 percent. Three times the industry average."

### Scene 4: What Actually Works
- **Visual**: Text overlay with each point appearing: "Subject lines that earn the click. Consistent schedule. Value density. Behavioral segmentation."
- **Audio**: "Stop fighting the tab. Start writing emails worth opening. The newsletters with the highest engagement don't escape Promotions — they make content good enough that subscribers find them there."

### Scene 5: The Real Fix
- **Visual**: Phone screen showing user dragging email from Promotions to Primary, with "Gmail learns" text overlay
- **Audio**: "Subscribers who care will train Gmail themselves. They drag, they reply, they add you to contacts. Your job is to give them a reason to."

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Write emails worth opening. Read the full breakdown — link in bio." TLDR logo fade in.
- **Audio**: "Write emails worth opening. Everything else follows. Full breakdown in the link."
