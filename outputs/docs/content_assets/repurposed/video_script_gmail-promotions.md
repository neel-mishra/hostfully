# Video Script: Gmail's Promotions Tab

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay on screen: "Your emails aren't broken." Quick zoom into a Gmail inbox showing the Promotions tab.
- **Audio**: "Your newsletter is landing in Gmail's Promotions tab — and you're panicking. Stop."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Misconception (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Split screen — left side shows "SPAM" folder with red X, right side shows "PROMOTIONS" tab with green checkmark. Animated arrows.
- **Audio**: "Promotions isn't spam. It's Gmail saying 'this looks like marketing.' Which... your newsletter is. Your email was delivered. Gmail just filed it."

### Scene 2: Why It Happens (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Animated list appearing on screen: bulk sending, HTML templates, link density, low reply rates. Each item appears with a small icon.
- **Audio**: "Gmail looks at five signals: bulk sending, HTML formatting, link density, reply rates, and template consistency. These describe every newsletter ever published. The algorithm isn't broken — it's working exactly as designed."

### Scene 3: What Actually Works (25-45s)
- **Visual**: Three cards flipping over one at a time: "REPLIES", "CONTACTS", "CONSISTENCY". Each with a brief stat.
- **Audio**: "Three things that actually move emails to Primary. One: replies. A single reply from a subscriber can reclassify your sender profile. Two: contact additions. Ask subscribers to add your email address in your welcome sequence. Three: consistency. TLDR sends to 7 million subscribers every weekday. 48% open rates. That reliability trains Gmail over time."

### Scene 4: The Real Fix (45-55s)
- **Visual**: Text on screen: "Stop gaming the algorithm. Start earning the inbox." Background transitions from dark to bright.
- **Audio**: "Stop googling deliverability hacks. The newsletters with the best placement aren't tricking Gmail. They're writing content people want to open. Better content. Cleaner lists. More engagement. That's the whole playbook."

## Call to Action (55-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Full breakdown in the blog — link in bio." TLDR logo fade-in.
- **Audio**: "Full breakdown in the blog post. Link in bio."
