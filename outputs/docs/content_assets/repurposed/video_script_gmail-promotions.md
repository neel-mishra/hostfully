# Video Script: Why Your Emails Land in Gmail Promotions

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (9:16) — TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
**Source:** Why-Your-Emails-Are-Going-to-Gmails-Promotions.md

---

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual:** Close-up of a phone screen showing the Gmail Promotions tab with dozens of unread emails. Text overlay slams in: "Promotions ≠ Spam."
- **Audio:** "Your emails aren't going to spam. Gmail is just filing them — and there's a difference."

## Setup (3–12s)
- **Visual:** Split-screen animation — left side shows a filing cabinet labeled "Promotions," right side shows a trash can labeled "Spam." The filing cabinet opens to show neatly organized emails. The trash can catches fire.
- **Audio:** "Gmail sorts mail for 1.8 billion accounts every day. Spam means your email got blocked — hidden, deleted in 30 days. Promotions means it got delivered and filed. One's a trash can. The other's a filing cabinet."

## Body (12–45s)
- **Scene 1 (12–20s):** Animated checklist appears on screen, each item checking off with a satisfying tick. Text overlay lists: bulk sending, templated design, scheduled cadence, promo language, image-heavy, low engagement.
  | **Audio:** "Gmail doesn't care if your content is good. It cares if it looks like marketing. Bulk sends, same template every time, scheduled delivery, words like 'free' and 'discount,' too many images, low engagement — hit four of those and you're in Promotions by default."

- **Scene 2 (20–28s):** Screen recording–style graphic showing the same email appearing in Primary for one user and Promotions for another. Arrows and labels highlight "engagement history" as the variable.
  | **Audio:** "Here's what most people miss. Gmail personalizes tabs per reader. The same email lands in Primary for one subscriber and Promotions for another. The difference is engagement. Opens, replies, clicks."

- **Scene 3 (28–36s):** Animated flywheel graphic — "Opens → Better Placement → More Visibility → More Opens" looping. Counter ticks up: "40–48% open rates. 7M+ subscribers."
  | **Audio:** "Open rates compound. Engaged readers train Gmail to prioritize you, which gets you more visibility, which gets you more engagement. We run 12 newsletters at TLDR with 40 to 48 percent open rates and over 7 million subscribers. We land in Promotions plenty. Still works."

- **Scene 4 (36–45s):** Quick-cut list on screen, each tip punching in with bold text: "1. Drag to Primary 2. Add to Contacts 3. Reply 4. Send Consistently."
  | **Audio:** "Four things that actually move the needle. Ask readers to drag your email to Primary — Gmail will apply it to future sends. Ask them to add you to contacts. Encourage replies. And send on a consistent schedule. That's it."

## Call to Action (45–60s)
- **Visual:** TLDR logo and newsletter sign-up URL on screen. Text overlay: "The Promotions tab isn't your enemy. Low engagement is." End card with "Follow for more" and a pointing-down animation toward the follow button.
- **Audio:** "Stop blaming the Promotions tab. Start sending emails people actually want to open. Follow for more on newsletters, growth, and email strategy — or hit the link in bio to subscribe to TLDR."

---

## Alt Hooks (test these)

1. "Gmail sorts 1.8 billion inboxes a day — and it's putting your emails exactly where it thinks they belong." *(Stat-first hook with a mild challenge to the viewer.)*
2. "We have 7 million email subscribers and our emails still land in Promotions. Here's why we don't care." *(Counterintuitive flex — subverts the panic narrative.)*
3. "If you think Promotions is the same as spam, you're solving the wrong problem." *(Direct confrontation — calls out a common misconception.)*
