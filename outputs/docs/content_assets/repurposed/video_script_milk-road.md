# Video Script: How Milk Road Sold for 7 Figures in 10 Months

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (9:16) — TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
**Source:** Milk-Road-From-0-to-Acquisition-in-10-months.md

---

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual:** Bold white text on black: "0 → 250K subs → 7-figure exit." Numbers animate in rapid sequence. No face yet — just the numbers.
- **Audio:** "Zero subscribers to a seven-figure acquisition. Ten months. Here's the actual playbook."

## Setup (3–12s)
- **Visual:** Cut to presenter (talking head, eye-level, natural lighting). Screen-record B-roll of a Milk Road newsletter issue overlaid mid-sentence.
- **Audio:** "Early 2022, Shaan Puri and Ben Levy launched a daily crypto newsletter called Milk Road. Crypto news was scattered across Discord, Twitter, Telegram — total chaos. Milk Road was five minutes, plain English, one email."

## Body (12–45s)

- **Scene 1 — The Model (12–20s):**
  - **Visual:** Split-screen graphic: left side shows a reader icon with "5 min daily email," right side shows an advertiser icon with "native ads, low CPCs." Arrows connect them.
  - **Audio:** "Newsletters are two-sided. Readers want signal. Advertisers want those readers. Milk Road locked in both sides early. The editorial team wrote the ads — so they didn't feel like ads. Readers actually read them."

- **Scene 2 — The Flywheel (20–28s):**
  - **Visual:** Animated circular flywheel graphic: Subscribers → Ad demand → Revenue → Growth budget → back to Subscribers. Each node lights up as it's mentioned.
  - **Audio:** "More subscribers meant more ad demand. More revenue meant more growth budget. That flywheel spun fast because they sequenced it right — growth first, monetization second, optimization third."

- **Scene 3 — The Tailwinds (28–37s):**
  - **Visual:** Three quick cuts: (1) screenshot of Shaan's Twitter follower count, (2) a referral program share screen, (3) a Bitcoin price chart from early 2022 trending upward.
  - **Audio:** "Three tailwinds made this possible. Shaan already had a massive audience from My First Million. Referral programs drove organic growth on top of paid. And early 2022 still had bull market energy — demand for crypto content was through the roof."

- **Scene 4 — The Lesson (37–45s):**
  - **Visual:** Back to presenter. Text overlay fades in: "The playbook, not the conditions." Presenter counts off on fingers.
  - **Audio:** "You can't copy their timing. But the principles work anywhere. Solve one problem clearly. Build both sides of the network. Sequence your priorities. And make ads part of the product."

## Call to Action (45–60s)
- **Visual:** Presenter on screen. End card slides in from right with TLDR logo and "Link in bio." Lower-third text: "Full breakdown on the TLDR blog."
- **Audio:** "TLDR does the same thing across 12 newsletters — 48% open rates, 7 million readers, and we write our advertisers' copy too. Full case study is on the blog. Link in bio. Follow for more breakdowns like this."

---

## Alt Hooks (test these)

1. "A crypto newsletter made seven figures in 10 months — and it wasn't even about trading." *(Curiosity gap — subverts the crypto-bro expectation.)*
2. "250,000 subscribers. Seven-figure exit. The founders spent zero dollars on content." *(Counterintuitive framing — makes people want to know how.)*
3. "Most newsletters take years to monetize. Milk Road did it in weeks." *(Direct challenge to conventional wisdom — strong scroll-stopper.)*
