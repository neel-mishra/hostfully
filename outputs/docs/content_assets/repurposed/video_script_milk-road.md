# Video Script: Milk Road — 0 to Acquisition in 10 Months

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline animation: "Feb 2022: 0 subscribers" fast-forwarding to "Dec 2022: 250K+ subscribers — ACQUIRED." Dramatic pause.
- **Audio**: "Zero subscribers. No team. No brand. Ten months later — acquired. Here's how Milk Road did it."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Cold DM
- **Visual**: Stylized Twitter DM notification appearing on screen. Text: "Want to start a crypto newsletter?" Ben Levy's silhouette with a question mark.
- **Audio**: "It started with a cold Twitter DM in February 2022. Ben Levy had nothing — no audience, no content, no infrastructure. Just a bet on rising crypto interest."

### Scene 2: The Positioning Play
- **Visual**: Two lanes on screen. Left: "Crypto-native" (complex charts, trading screens). Right: "Crypto-curious" (friendly, accessible design). Arrow points right. Text: "Serve the periphery."
- **Audio**: "The key decision: serve the crypto-curious, not the crypto-native. While competitors wrote token analysis for traders, Milk Road wrote a daily briefing for people who just wanted to understand."

### Scene 3: The Growth Stack
- **Visual**: Four blocks stacking up like building blocks. Each labeled: "Referrals" → "Cross-promotion" → "Paid ads" → "SEO." A bar chart growing behind them.
- **Audio**: "Growth came from stacking channels. A one-referral, one-reward program. Newsletter cross-promotion. Meme-style Facebook and TikTok ads. And SEO content that drove signups months after publishing. No single channel was more than 40 percent."

### Scene 4: What Made Them Acquirable
- **Visual**: Three gold badges appearing: "Engagement Quality" → "Mainstream Audience" → "Simple Operations." Each with a checkmark.
- **Audio**: "Subscriber count alone doesn't close deals. Three things made them acquirable: verified engagement, a mainstream audience that's hard to assemble, and simple operations any acquirer could integrate."

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "The playbook is timing-independent." TLDR blog link below.
- **Audio**: "Timing helped. But the playbook works in any niche. Full breakdown on the TLDR blog."
