# Video Script: Milk Road — Zero to Acquisition in 10 Months

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline animation: "0 subscribers" on left, arrow spanning "10 months", "ACQUIRED" on right. Fast zoom.
- **Audio**: "A cold Twitter DM turned into a newsletter acquisition in under a year. Here's the playbook."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Setup (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Twitter DM notification animation. Crypto news headlines scrolling in background. "Early 2022" text.
- **Audio**: "Ben Levy and his co-founders launched Milk Road in 2022. Zero subscribers. Zero brand. Zero content. They bet that a daily crypto newsletter — entertaining, accessible, fast — could cut through a crowded market."

### Scene 2: The Five Levers (15-40s)
- **Visual**: Five lever icons appearing one at a time, each with a brief stat/label underneath.
- **Audio**: "Five growth levers drove 250K subscribers. One: a dead-simple referral program. One referral equals one crypto investor PDF. No tiers, no merch, fully automated. Two: cross-promotion with other newsletters. Borrowing trust at scale. Three: paid ads on Facebook and TikTok — memes and social proof creative — but they tracked unsubscribe rates per channel, not just signups. If a channel brought subscribers who immediately left, they killed the spend. Four: embedded polls in every issue. Polls turn passive readers into active participants. Five: no-code tools for everything. They iterated weekly, not quarterly."

### Scene 3: Why It Got Acquired (40-50s)
- **Visual**: Four pillars rising: "Repeatable Growth", "Engaged Audience", "Revenue Model", "Lean Ops". Building/stacking animation.
- **Audio**: "The acquisition happened because the fundamentals were unusually strong. Repeatable growth across multiple channels — not a viral spike. An engaged audience with real open rates, not vanity metrics. Clear revenue potential from a valuable crypto demographic. And a lean team with automated infrastructure."

### Scene 4: The Takeaway (50-55s)
- **Visual**: Text on screen: "No magic. No viral moment. Just discipline." Clean, minimal.
- **Audio**: "The formula isn't complicated. Clear positioning. Aggressive distribution. Data discipline. Relentless engagement. Compounded over 10 months."

## Call to Action (55-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Full case study breakdown — link in bio." TLDR logo.
- **Audio**: "Full case study linked below. These principles work in any niche, not just crypto."
