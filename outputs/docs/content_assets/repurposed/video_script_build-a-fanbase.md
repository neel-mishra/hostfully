# Video Script: How To Build a Fanbase (Not Just a Following)

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (9:16) — TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
**Source:** How-To-Build-a-Fanbase-From-Followers-to-True-Fans.md

---

## Hook (0–3s)
- **Visual:** Black screen. White text punches in word by word: "You don't need a million followers." Hard cut to host, mid-frame, talking straight to camera.
- **Audio:** "You don't need a million followers. You need a thousand fans."

## Setup (3–12s)
- **Visual:** Host walking or standing, casual setting (desk / studio / street). Cut to screen recording showing a social media profile with 500K followers but 12 likes on a post.
- **Audio:** "Kevin Kelly wrote about this in 2008. A thousand true fans — people who buy everything you make, share your stuff without being asked, and actually stick around — that's enough to build a career. Ten thousand? That's a company."

## Body (12–42s)

- **Scene 1:** Quick-cut montage of follower count tickers spinning up, intercut with a "likes" counter barely moving. Text overlay: "Followers ≠ Fans."
- **Audio:** "But most creators chase the wrong number. Followers are algorithm artifacts. The platform gave them to you. The platform can take them back."

- **Scene 2:** Screen recording of an Instagram or TikTok account getting suspended / notification saying "account restricted." Text overlay: "You don't own this."
- **Audio:** "If your entire audience lives on Instagram or TikTok, you don't have a fanbase. You have a lease. And the landlord doesn't care about you."

- **Scene 3:** Phone screen showing an email inbox with a newsletter open. Cut to stat card: "TLDR: 7M+ subscribers. 48% open rate." Animated underline on "48%."
- **Audio:** "TLDR built a newsletter with over seven million subscribers and a 48% open rate. The industry average is 15 to 20. The difference? They own the relationship. Email, not algorithm."

- **Scene 4:** Host back on camera. Holds up three fingers, counts down. Text overlays appear one at a time: "Consistency. Honesty. Evolution."
- **Audio:** "Three things keep fans around. Consistency — show up when you said you would. Honesty — don't pretend to be something you're not. Evolution — grow, or they'll outgrow you."

## Call to Action (42–60s)
- **Visual:** Host on camera, leaning in slightly. End card with text: "Stop renting. Start owning." Below it: "Link in bio" or handle.
- **Audio:** "Social media is a funnel. It's not a foundation. Use it to find people, then move them somewhere you control — an email list, a community, a direct line. Stop counting followers. Start counting fans. Link in bio if you want the full breakdown."

---

## Alt Hooks (test these)

1. "48% open rate with seven million subscribers. Most newsletters get 15. Here's why." *(leads with the stat — pattern interrupt for anyone in the creator/marketing space)*
2. "Your followers aren't yours. Here's proof." *(creates an open loop — viewers need to hear the proof)*
3. "One thousand fans is worth more than a million followers. I'll explain in 60 seconds." *(Kevin Kelly's thesis as a bold claim + time constraint builds urgency)*
