# Video Script: How to Build a Fanbase — Followers vs. True Fans

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Counter showing "50,000 followers" on the left with a sad face emoji, "2,000 true fans" on the right with a rocket emoji. Text overlay: "Which one builds a business?"
- **Audio**: "50,000 followers or 2,000 true fans. Only one of these builds a real business."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Kevin Kelly Thesis (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Animated text — "1,000 True Fans" appearing in bold, then transforming into a sustainable business icon. Kevin Kelly quote card.
- **Audio**: "Kevin Kelly argued you need 1,000 true fans to build a sustainable business. Most creators misread this as 'grow slowly.' The real lesson? Stop optimizing for reach. Start optimizing for depth."

### Scene 2: What Creators Get Wrong (15-30s)
- **Visual**: Three mistakes appearing as crossed-out cards: "Vanity metrics" (follower counter), "Rented land" (Instagram/Twitter logos on a shaky foundation), "Ignoring early supporters" (notification bell with dust).
- **Audio**: "Mistake one: chasing follower counts that don't convert. Mistake two: building your entire audience on platforms you don't control — one algorithm change and you lose everything. Mistake three: treating subscriber number 1 the same as subscriber 50,000. Your first 500 people are your unpaid marketing team."

### Scene 3: The Playbook (30-45s)
- **Visual**: Step-by-step visual — Email icon (owned channel), Arrow to Newsletter, Arrow to Community. Data callout: "TLDR: 1.6M subs, 48% open rate."
- **Audio**: "Own the relationship through email. It's the only channel where you control distribution. Use social media as a funnel, not a foundation. Reward early supporters — give them exclusive access, ask for their input, make them feel like co-founders. TLDR's flagship has 1.6 million subscribers with 48 percent open rates. That engagement ratio is what matters."

### Scene 4: The Core Truth (45-52s)
- **Visual**: Clean text: "Depth > Breadth" with a single strong line underneath.
- **Audio**: "The creators with the strongest fanbases have one thing in common: they respect their audience's time and intelligence. Everything else follows from that."

## Call to Action (52-60s)
- **Visual**: Blog post card with "Full playbook on the TLDR blog" and subscribe CTA. TLDR logo.
- **Audio**: "Full breakdown on turning followers into true fans — link in bio. And if you want to see this playbook in action, subscribe to TLDR. 5 minutes a day, free."
