# Video Script: Milk Road — Zero to Acquisition in 10 Months

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline graphic showing "Day 1: Launch" on the left, "Month 10: Acquired" on the right, with rapid growth line between them. Dollar sign appears at the end.
- **Audio**: "A newsletter went from zero to acquired in 10 months. Here's exactly how they did it."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Opportunity (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Gap visualization — crypto professionals drowning in information on one side, clean 5-minute briefing on the other. Milk Road logo appears.
- **Audio**: "Milk Road's founders saw a simple gap. Crypto professionals needed a daily briefing — not a 45-minute podcast, not Twitter doomscrolling. Five minutes of what matters. The format was borrowed from TLDR and Morning Brew. The niche was growing fast enough to compress every timeline."

### Scene 2: The Growth Machine (15-30s)
- **Visual**: Three growth channels appearing as pillars: "Paid Acquisition" (ad icons), "Referrals" (network graph), "SEO" (search bar). Subscriber counter ticking up rapidly.
- **Audio**: "They ran three growth channels in parallel. Sharp paid acquisition on LinkedIn and Meta. A referral program where subscribers recruited new ones — referred subscribers convert at higher rates than paid. And SEO content for organic, compounding growth. Every new subscriber made the ad inventory more valuable."

### Scene 3: The Business Model (30-42s)
- **Visual**: Newsletter mockup with three ad slots highlighted — Top (primary), Middle, Bottom (quick links). Revenue meter showing "52x ROI" for sponsors. Text: "3 ads max per issue."
- **Audio**: "The ad model: three sponsor slots per newsletter — that's it. Primary placement at the top, mid-newsletter slot, and quick links at the bottom. The cap at three creates scarcity, which drives premium pricing. The editorial team wrote sponsor copy to match the newsletter voice. Some sponsors reported 52x ROI."

### Scene 4: The Flywheel (42-52s)
- **Visual**: Animated flywheel spinning — "More Readers → Better Ads → More Revenue → Better Content → More Readers." Speed increases. Open rate counter: "40-48%."
- **Audio**: "The flywheel: more readers made ads more valuable. More ad revenue funded better content and faster growth. Better content attracted more readers. Once spinning, this cycle compounds. Open rates stayed at 40 to 48 percent — well above the 15 to 20 percent industry average."

## Call to Action (52-60s)
- **Visual**: Text card: "The full acquisition playbook" with blog link and TLDR logo. "Same model. 12 newsletters. 7M+ subscribers."
- **Audio**: "The same model powers TLDR across 12 newsletters and 7 million subscribers. Full deep dive on the Milk Road acquisition on the TLDR blog. Link in bio."
