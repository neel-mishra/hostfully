# Video Script: Milk Road — 0 to Acquisition in 10 Months

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline graphic rapidly filling from "Day 1" to "Month 10" with dollar signs appearing at the end. "8 FIGURES" text slams onto screen.
- **Audio**: "A crypto newsletter went from zero to an eight-figure acquisition in ten months. Here's exactly how."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1: The Gap
- **Visual**: Cluttered social media feed with crypto jargon, then a clean email inbox with one simple newsletter
- **Audio**: "Crypto was drowning in noise. Too technical for mainstream readers. Too shallow to be useful. Milk Road bet on one thing: a daily email that explains crypto in five minutes."

### Scene 2: The Growth Engines
- **Visual**: Three growth levers appearing as animated gears: "Referrals," "Cross-promotions," "Paid acquisition"
- **Audio**: "Automated referral programs turned subscribers into a free growth engine. Cross-promotions with adjacent newsletters brought warm audiences. Paid ads tracked quality — open rates and retention, not just cost per subscriber."

### Scene 3: Why It Worked
- **Visual**: Graph showing open rates climbing above 40% line, with "Industry avg: 15-20%" shown below
- **Audio**: "Daily publishing built habits. Habits built open rates above 40 percent. That's double the industry average. High open rates made them irresistible to advertisers."

### Scene 4: The Acquisition Math
- **Visual**: Two newsletter icons side by side — "250K subs, 45% opens" with green checkmark vs "1M subs, 12% opens" with red X
- **Audio**: "Newsletter acquisitions value audience quality over size. A 250,000 subscriber list with 45 percent open rates is worth more than a million subscribers at 12 percent."

### Scene 5: The Lesson
- **Visual**: Five bullet points appearing rapidly: "Pick a niche. Ship daily. Automate growth. Track quality. Move fast."
- **Audio**: "Pick a niche and own it. Ship daily. Automate your growth loops. Track quality, not volume. And move fast — Milk Road didn't wait for perfection."

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "The Milk Road story isn't about crypto. It's about execution." TLDR logo and link.
- **Audio**: "This isn't a crypto story. It's an execution story. Full case study breakdown in the link."
