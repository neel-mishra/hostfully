## What Worked

1. **Mexico/Colombia prospecting via Meta** — At $115.82 CPL, this geo delivers leads at roughly 5x the efficiency of US/CA prospecting. The property management interest + job title targeting combination works well in LATAM markets.

2. **CA-US retargeting** — $80.53 CPL demonstrates that warming audiences through the funnel before conversion drives much better unit economics than cold prospecting in the same market.

3. **Content pipeline velocity on high-score items** — All items with weighted scores of 3.2+ were completed, showing the prioritization framework is working.

4. **Video creative performance** — 234,292 video views across campaigns suggest video ads are driving meaningful top-of-funnel engagement.

## What Didn't Work

1. **CA-US cold prospecting at scale** — $568.31 CPL on $17K spend. This is the largest budget allocation but produces the worst CPL among lead-generating campaigns. The US/CA market is saturated and expensive for cold audiences in property management.

2. **AU-UK retargeting** — Zero leads on $425 spend. The retargeting pool in Australia/UK is likely too small or too exhausted to generate conversions. Budget is being wasted on engagement without conversion.

3. **Hostfully pt-br meeting campaign** — $458.46 CPL. While messaging connections (12) are being generated, they aren't converting to leads efficiently. The meeting-focused objective may be misaligned for a LATAM market that responds better to website conversion campaigns.

4. **Google Ads channel entirely offline** — The account credential issue means zero spend and zero data from what should be a complementary search acquisition channel.

5. **Content pipeline stalling on lower-score items** — 11 items remain not started, all scoring 2.2 or below. Without a forcing function, these will continue to accumulate.

## Learnings & Implications

1. **Geo arbitrage is the clearest lever.** LATAM markets (MX/CO/BR) deliver leads at a fraction of US/CA cost. June strategy should shift budget allocation toward these geos while maintaining retargeting in US/CA for warm audiences.

2. **Cold vs warm audience economics are clear.** Retargeting CPL ($80.53) vs prospecting CPL ($568.31) in the same geo (US/CA) is a 7x difference. The funnel should be designed to move more people into retargeting pools through content, engagement, and video view campaigns rather than driving direct cold-to-lead conversion.

3. **Fix Google Ads immediately.** Running a single-platform paid strategy exposes the business to platform risk and misses the intent-based search audience that Meta can't capture.

4. **Content pipeline needs a forcing function for mid-tier items.** The team executes well on high-priority content but stalls on items below the 2.6 threshold. Consider batching 2-3 lower-score items per sprint to maintain velocity.

5. **Hostfully pt-br should pivot from meeting-focused to website-conversion optimization** to match the objective structure that's working in the MX campaign.

6. **AU-UK retargeting should be paused** until the audience pool is rebuilt through prospecting campaigns in that geo.
