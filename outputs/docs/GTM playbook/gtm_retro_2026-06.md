## What Worked

1. **Retargeting efficiency:** Both CA-US and AU-UK retargeting campaigns show CPLs 3-5x better than prospecting. Warm audiences convert at dramatically lower cost.
2. **Mexico prospecting:** Best CPL among prospecting campaigns ($140.41) suggests the MX PMS market is less saturated with lower competition costs.
3. **Video creative approach:** 204K video views indicate strong creative resonance. The pt-br campaign leverages video heavily with $0.79 CPC.
4. **Content production velocity:** 9 articles completed demonstrates functional content pipeline, with focus on actionable newsletter growth content that aligns with TLDR's audience.

---

## What Didn't Work

1. **CA-US prospecting at scale:** $17K spend with $532 CPL. The LAL 1% ideal-5to30 audience may be exhausting. Audience quality doesn't justify the premium.
2. **EU prospecting volume:** Only 9 leads from $3K spend indicates the EU PMS market may require different messaging or targeting approach.
3. **Content backlog stalling:** 12 of 21 items untouched. The "Not Started" items include potentially high-impact pieces (Milk Road deep dive, Gmail deliverability) that remain unaddressed.
4. **Data visibility gaps:** Google Ads and Ahrefs credentials not configured, creating blind spots in cross-channel performance assessment.

---

## Learnings & Implications for Next Month

1. **Reallocate budget from CA-US prospecting to retargeting:** The 5x CPL gap between prospecting and retargeting in CA-US suggests retargeting budget should be increased while prospecting is paused or restructured.

2. **Double down on Mexico:** $140 CPL at volume makes MX the most efficient prospecting market. Consider increasing daily budget from $250 to $400+.

3. **Test new CA-US prospecting audiences:** The current LAL 1% ideal-5to30 audience is expensive. Test broader interest-based audiences, different lookalike seeds, or Advantage+ audiences.

4. **Fix data infrastructure:** Priority should be given to configuring Google Ads credentials (fix token refresh) and adding Ahrefs API key to enable full-funnel visibility.

5. **Unblock content backlog:** The Milk Road deep dive and Gmail Promotions articles are high-potential pieces that should be prioritized for July production.

6. **Attribution clarity:** With 204K video views and 115 leads, understanding which creative formats actually drive conversions (not just engagement) should inform creative strategy.
