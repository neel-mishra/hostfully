# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Fast cuts of email inboxes overflowing, then someone slamming their fist on a desk in frustration. Quick zoom in on a confused/stressed face.
- **Audio**: "Newsletters flopping?!" (Upbeat, frustrated tone)

## Body (Main Content)
- [Scene 1]: **Visual:** Text overlay: "2026 Newsletter REVAMP Checklist!" | **Narration:** "STOP! Boost your newsletter engagement with these hacks!" (Voice speeds up/gains energy)
- [Scene 2]: **Visual:** Animated graphic of subject line with text quickly flashing: "Under 50 chars," "Lead with the fact," "Killer preheader!" | **Narration:** "SUBJECT LINES! Keep 'em short, ditch the clickbait, and treat the preheader like GOLD."
- [Scene 3]: **Visual:** Animated graphic on a screen saying Content, then zoom in phrases like: "Synthesis!", "POV!", "Niche" | **Narration:** "CONTENT! Synthesis OVER summary, nail your POV, and write for a NICHE, not the world."
- [Scene 4]: **Visual:** Quick shots of happy faces replying to comments, sharing posts, and clicking referral links. | **Narration:** "COMMUNITY! Set rules, encourage support, and make sharing EASY with ONE-CLICK referrals!" (Voice builds excitement)
- [Scene 5]: **Visual:** Text overlay: "CONSISTENCY = GROWTH" and an arrow pointing upwards. | **Narration:** "Consistency compounds, friends! Focus on ONE of these each week for BIG results!"

## Call to Action (55-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Which one are YOU tackling this week? Let me know in comments!" Animated pointing hand emoji.
- **Audio**: "Which one are YOU crushing this week? Drop it in the comments!" (Enthusiastic, challenging tone)
