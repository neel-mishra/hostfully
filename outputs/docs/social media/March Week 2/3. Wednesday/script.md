- **Scene 3**: [Visual] Quick cuts of positive comments and testimonials flashing on screen. | [Narration] "Only 3 ads per issue, total transparency, and readers who *actually* pay attention."

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "See Case Studies & CPC Comparisons 👇". Arrow pointing to the comments section.
