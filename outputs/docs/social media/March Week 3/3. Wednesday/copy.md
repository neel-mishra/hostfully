From 0 to a local powerhouse in 66 days. 📍

Take the 'Elmwood District' example: 
Instead of a general 'Portland News' feed, a resident started a niche newsletter specifically for their neighborhood. 

They didn't focus on 1,000,000 readers. They focused on the 500 people who cared about:
✅ School board updates
✅ New zoning changes
✅ Local business spotlights

By day 66, they hit profitability through local sponsorships. Why? Because local businesses don't want 'reach'—they want their neighbors.

Small is the new big.
