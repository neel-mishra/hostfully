## LinkedIn Post Copy

5 Mistakes that are killing your newsletter engagement (and how to fix them): 

1️⃣ The 'Overnight' Send: Sending while your readers sleep. 
✅ Fix: Aim for the 'Early Morning Peak' when professionals check their inbox first thing.

2️⃣ Mismatched Swaps: Partnering with anyone who has a pulse.
✅ Fix: Deep-dive into audience demographics before agreeing to a collab.

3️⃣ The 'Generalist' Trap: Trying to be everything to everyone.
✅ Fix: Define your niche geographic or professional area. Be indispensable to a few.

4️⃣ Ignoring the ROI: Treating your newsletter as a hobby, not an asset.
✅ Fix: Track your CPC and subscriber LTV from day one.

5️⃣ Over-Promoting: Turning your content into a digital flyer.
✅ Fix: 80% Value, 20% Ask.
