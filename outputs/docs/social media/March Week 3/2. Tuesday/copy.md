## LinkedIn Post Copy

Stop burning your budget on Meta ads for newsletter growth. 🛑

Paid acquisition is a treadmill. The moment you stop spending, the growth dies. Worse? The leads are often 'cold' and churn within 30 days.

There is a better way: The Newsletter Swap.

Instead of paying a platform, you leverage trust. By partnering with creators who share your audience, you inherit their credibility. 

Key rules for a successful swap:
1. Audience Alignment > Audience Size. 
2. Value over Promotion (Don't just pitch; provide a reason to care).
3. Respect the Inbox (Over-promoting kills trust).

The best growth isn't bought; it’s borrowed through shared value.

What’s your take: Paid ads or organic swaps? 👇
