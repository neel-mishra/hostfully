# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Quick time-lapse of someone typing furiously at a computer, piles of papers accumulating, then a calendar filling up with checkmarks. Upbeat, fast-paced music.
- **Audio**: (Energetic voice) "Want 55% email open rates? I’ll give you the secret!"

## Body (Main Content)
- **Scene 1:** [Visual / B-Roll] Short clip of someone looking frustrated looking at "get rich quick" pop ups. Alternatively, search results for "email hacks." | (Narration) "It's NOT magic. It's NOT a perfect emoji. And it's DEFINITELY not some clever clickbait!"
- **Scene 2:** [Visual] Calendar app showing consistent daily entries - ideally the view where you see a full month of dots. B-roll of someone thoughtfully writing. | (Narration) "It's showing up. Every. Single. Weekday. For YEARS!"
- **Scene 3:** [Visual] Graphic with the text: Year 1: Build the Routine. Year 2: Build the Trust. Year 3: Build the Community. Accompanying visuals of someone working hard, someone smiling and high-fiving, and a lively online forum. | (Narration) "Consistency COMPOUNDS! Forget chasing viral. Build long-term value."
- **Scene 4:** [Visual] Screenshot of positive email replies, people saying thank you, etc. | (Narration) "When they know YOUR name means VALUE, the subject line doesn't even matter!

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Stop the shortcuts. Start building the habit!"
- **Audio**: (Upbeat, encouraging) "Ditch the hacks. Build something real. Let's GO!"
