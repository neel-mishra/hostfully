## LinkedIn Post Copy

Unpopular opinion: Clickbait is a high-interest debt that will eventually bankrupt your brand.

'Curiosity gaps' might get you a one-time open, but if the content doesn't deliver a revelation, you’ve just taught your subscriber not to trust you.

In 2026, the 'hacks' are actually hurting you:

❌ Excessive Emojis: Using 🚀✨🎉 triggers spam filters and looks desperate.
❌ Fake Urgency: Using 'RE:' or 'Urgent' when it’s not creates immediate resentment.
❌ Generic Personalization: 'Dear [First Name]' is a signal of low-effort automation.

The Fix? Clarity over cleverness.

'Apple’s WWDC was 90% AI' beats 'You won't believe what Apple just did' every single time.

Stop trying to 'trick' people into opening. Start giving them a reason to want to.
