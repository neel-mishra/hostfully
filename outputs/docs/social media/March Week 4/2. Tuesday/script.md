# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Fast cuts of email subject lines like "RE:", "Urgent", and overly enthusiastic emojis. Overlay text: "Clickbait = Brand Bankruptcy!"
- **Audio**: (Upbeat, slightly aggressive music starts) Voiceover: "Clickbait is KILLING your brand!"

## Body (Main Content)
- **Scene 1**: [Visual / B-roll]: Quick shots of someone deleting emails on their phone, annoyed faces. | Voiceover: "Those 'amazing' subject lines? They're high-interest debt. One click, but you're making withdrawals from your credibility bank!"
- **Scene 2**: [Visual]: Graphic of a red "X" over emojis like rockets, stars, and party poppers dropping into a spam filter. Then, a red "X" over email software with fake urgency headlines. Then, a hand quickly changing a first name in a generic email template.| Voiceover: "2024 called, and it wants its clickbait back! Excessive emojis? Spam filters! Fake urgency? Instant resentment! Generic personalization? LAZY!"
- **Scene 3**: [Visual]: Split screen. One side has a clickbait headline (e.g., "You WON'T BELIEVE..."); the other side has a clear headline (e.g., "Apple WWDC: 90% AI"). The clear headline side is highlighted. | Voiceover: "Clarity over cleverness! 'Apple’s WWDC was 90% AI' - that's VALUE, not a trick!"

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "STOP THE CLICKBAIT!" with an upwards pointing arrow toward the like/comment buttons. Green check mark appearing next to the word Clarity.
- **Audio**: "Stop tricking, start giving value! Agree or disagree? Drop your thoughts in the comments!" (Music fades slightly but keeps energy)
