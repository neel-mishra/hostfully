# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Close-up of a phone screen overflowing with unopened emails. A hand dramatically swipes across the screen deleting them rapidly. Sound of deleting/swooshing.
- **Audio**: "STOP KILLING YOUR EMAIL OPEN RATES!" (Fast-paced, energetic delivery)

## Body (Main Content)
- **[Scene 1]: [Visual / B-roll]**: Text animation: "7 Deadly Subject Line Sins" with a fiery background. Visual of a long subject line getting cut off in a phone notification. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #1: Subject lines over 50 characters. Mobile views will cut you off...mid-sentence!"
- **[Scene 2]: [Visual]**: Text animation, flashing "ALL CAPS BAD". Visual of an angry face emoji overlaid on an email. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #2: ALL CAPS! It screams 'spam' and annoys everyone."
- **[Scene 3]: [Visual]**: Brief clip of a disguised advertisement, then a "NO!" graphic appears over it. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #3: Disguised ads. No one likes feeling tricked. Instant trust destroyer!"
- **[Scene 4]: [Visual]**: Side-by-side examples: "Synergize your workflow" (blurry, unclear) vs. "3 Productivity Hacks" (sharp, clear). | **[Narration]**: "Sin #4: Vague language. Be clear! Tell them exactly what's inside."
- **[Scene 5]: [Visual]**: Phone screen highlighting the preheader text. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #5: Ignoring the preheader! Free real estate - use it!"
- **[Scene 6]: [Visual]**: A calendar flipping through random dates, animation showing inconsistent send times. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #6: Sending emails at random times. Consistency builds habits!"
- **[Scene 7]: [Visual]**: Phone screen showing "Hi [First_Name]". Zoom in on the bracketed text with an angry face emoji overlay. | **[Narration]**: "Sin #7: BROKEN MERGE TAGS! Ugh. Nothing's worse than 'Hi bracket First_Name'!"

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: "SAVE THIS! What's the WORST sin you see?" (Text overlay with arrows pointing to a comment section)
- **Audio**: "Which one of these makes you cringe the most? Tell me in the comments! Don't forget to SAVE this for your next email campaign!" (Fast spoken, upbeat music fades out)
