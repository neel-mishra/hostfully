## LinkedIn Post Copy

7 Deadly Sins of Email Subject Lines in 2026 🛑

If you want to hit 40%+ open rates, stop doing these immediately:

1. Going over 50 characters: Most opens are on mobile. If it's too long, the 'hook' gets truncated.
2. ALL CAPS: It screams amateurism and triggers spam filters.
3. Disguised Ads: Disguising sponsored content as editorial destroys trust instantly.
4. Ambiguity: 'Synergize your workflow' (Vague) vs. '3 Productivity Hacks for Remote Teams' (Clear).
5. Neglecting the Preheader: That preview text is free real estate. Treat it as a second headline.
6. Inconsistency: Sending at random times breaks the reader's routine. Habits drive opens.
7. Broken Merge Tags: Nothing kills a vibe like 'Hi [First_Name].'
