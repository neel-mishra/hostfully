## LinkedIn Post Copy

An audience consumes content passively. A community builds it with you.

Last year, we realized that having 10,000 subscribers meant nothing if they weren't talking to each other. We had a broadcast, not a hub.

Real community happens when:

1. Members start peer-to-peer support (solving each other's problems).
2. They develop a shared language and 'inside jokes.'
3. They look forward to the comments as much as the content.

To move from 'Audience' to 'Community,' you need a clear purpose. What is the one problem you solve? What is the shared passion that unites them?

If you're just broadcasting, you're building on rented land. If you're building a community, you're building a home.

Are you building an audience or a community?
