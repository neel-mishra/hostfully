## LinkedIn Post Copy

The average newsletter open rate is 15-20%.

But top-performers like Hostfully and Morning Brew regularly clear 50%.

They aren’t using 'magic' hacks. They are executing a 3-part framework that most creators ignore:

1. Trust is the Foundation
If you promise a 5-minute read, it better be 5 minutes. Consistency is the ultimate trust-builder. Sending at the same time every day builds a habit that subject lines alone can't touch.

2. Synthesis > Summarization
Anyone can link to 5 articles. High-open newsletters read those articles, extract the one thing that matters, and explain the 'so what.' If your reader can get the same value from scanning headlines, you’ve already lost.

3. Radical Specificity
Don't write for 'professionals.' Write for 'Senior DevOps Engineers at Series B Startups.' When you know exactly who is on the other side, your editorial decisions become sharper and your value becomes undeniable.

High open rates aren't a trick. They are the result of showing up and delivering on your promise, every single time.

Which of these 3 are you focusing on this week?
