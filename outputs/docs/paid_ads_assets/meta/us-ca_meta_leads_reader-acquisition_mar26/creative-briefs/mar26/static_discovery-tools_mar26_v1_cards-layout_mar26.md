## 2. Concept Overview

**Concept Name:** Tool Cards – “What Everyone Will Talk About Next Week”

**Description:** A card-based layout that shows 3 “tool cards” representing the kind of links TLDR surfaces each day. Each card has a simple name, one-line benefit, and tag (e.g., “AI”, “Dev Tool”, “Growth”). Above the cards: a headline promising early access to the best tools and ideas.

**Visual Hook:** The viewer quickly recognizes this as a curated list of interesting, actionable things — not generic news.

**On-Image Copy:**
- **Headline:** “New AI & dev tools, every morning.”
- **Subhead:** “Discover what everyone will talk about next week.”
- **Card examples (approximate):**
  - Card 1: “PromptSmith – Better prompts in half the time” (Tag: “AI”)
  - Card 2: “ShipCheck – Lightweight feature flags for side projects” (Tag: “Dev Tool”)
  - Card 3: “GrowthLoop – Tiny experiments that actually move numbers” (Tag: “Growth”)
- **CTA badge:** “Get TLDR free”

(Use fictional tool names unless you have real examples you want to feature.)

---

## 3. Sizing & Layout

### 4:5 Feed Portrait (Hero)

- Top 30%: Headline + subhead, left-aligned.
- Middle 60%: Three horizontal cards:
  - Card body: white panel with subtle shadow.
  - Top row: tool name.
  - Second row: one-line benefit.
  - Bottom row: small pill tag (AI / Dev Tool / Growth).
- Bottom 10%: CTA badge: “Get TLDR free”.

### 1:1 Feed Square

- Stack cards in 2 rows:
  - Top row: 2 cards.
  - Bottom row: 1 centered card + CTA beneath.
- Slightly reduce copy length if needed to keep legible on mobile.

### 9:16 Stories / Reels

- Vertical stack:
  - Headline at top, subhead just below.
  - 3 cards stacked with small spacing.
  - CTA badge near bottom within safe zone.

Ensure all text is comfortably within safe content zones and large enough to read in mobile feed.

## 5. Content & Craft Notes

- Cards should look like **editorial recommendations**, not product ads:
  - No brand logos unless approved.
  - Focus on descriptive, benefit-led titles.
- Use subtle motion in animated versions (e.g., slight hover on cards, gentle entrance) but keep static export simple.
- Avoid overwhelming the viewer with small text; each card should be skimmable in < 1 second.

---

## 6. Nano Banana Prompt (Optional Background Texture)

Minimal abstract tech background with soft overlapping rounded rectangles and subtle gradients in TLDR blue and cool neutrals. High-key, clean, editorial feel. No text, no logos, no UI elements, just soft shapes.
