# Ad Creative – us-ca_meta_leads_reader-acquisition_mar26 (Mar 2026)

This file contains March 2026 ad creative for the TLDR reader-acquisition Meta campaign targeting consumer newsletter subscribers in the US & Canada with a \$10,000/month budget.

## Creative Brief (Condensed)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Network Side** | Reader acquisition (newsletter subscribers) |
| **Campaign** | `us-ca_meta_leads_reader-acquisition_mar26` |
| **Objective** | Drive new TLDR tech newsletter signups from tech-curious consumers/prosumers |
| **Primary Persona** | Builders, operators, and tech-curious professionals (engineers, PMs, founders, growth, data) |
| **Key Message** | “Keep up with tech, startups, and AI in 5 minutes a day — no fluff.” |
| **Number of Core Angles** | 4 (Time Saved, Social Proof, Discovery/Tools, Noise vs Signal) |
| **Brand Voice** | Confident, sharp, practical; no hype or clickbait; talks to readers as peers |
| **Visual Direction** | Reader-facing: bright, blue, clean; show the newsletter UI, inbox, or real screenshots where possible |
| **Formats** | Static 1:1 + 4:5, vertical video 9:16 (Reels/Stories), optionally carousel |
| **Primary CTA** | “Sign Up” / “Get the newsletter” |
| **Funnel** | Mix of cold prospecting + warm retargeting |

## Angle 1 – Time Saved (“5-Minute Briefing”)

**Pillar:** Time Reclaimed  
**Use for:** Broad / Advantage+ and interest-based prospecting.

### Static Ad: `static_time-saved_mar26_v1`

- **Primary Text (Prospecting)**  
  “Too busy to wade through tech Twitter and a dozen blogs? TLDR gives you the 5‑minute briefing founders and operators actually read every morning.”

- **Primary Text (Retargeting)**  
  “You’ve already seen TLDR around. Make it official — get the 5‑minute briefing in your inbox before your first meeting.”

- **Headline Options (choose 1–2 per ad):**
  1. “The 5‑Minute Tech Briefing”
  2. “Stay on Top of Tech in One Email”
  3. “Read This Before Standup”

- **Description (optional):**  
  “Free daily email with the important stories, tools, and ideas in tech.”

- **Visual Notes:**  
  - Show a clean screenshot of the TLDR email on desktop or mobile, with a few highlighted sections.  
  - Overlay a small badge: “5‑minute read” or “Daily, no fluff.”  
  - Keep backgrounds simple; avoid generic “stock office” imagery.

  1. “Join the TLDR Reader Community”
  2. “The Newsletter Tech People Actually Read”
  3. “Your Peers Already Get This Email”

  “Thousands of builders get TLDR for free every morning.”

- **Visual Notes:**  
  - Social proof elements:  
    - Subscriber count (if you’re comfortable revealing it).

  - Make “Join TLDR” or “Free newsletter” visually explicit.

## Angle 3 – Discovery & Tools (“What Everyone Will Talk About Next Week”)

**Pillar:** Curated Signal / Discovery  
**Use for:** Interest targeting (tech, AI, SaaS) + LALs.

### Static Ad: `static_discovery-tools_mar26_v1`

- **Primary Text (Prospecting)**  
  “Every issue of TLDR highlights the most interesting AI tools, dev tricks, and growth experiments — before your feed is full of them.”

- **Primary Text (Retargeting)**  
  “Keep missing the cool new tools until they’re already everywhere? TLDR surfaces them while they’re still early.”

- **Headline Options:**
  1. “New AI & Dev Tools, Daily”
  2. “Discover What Everyone Will Talk About Next Week”
  3. “Stop Finding Out About Trends Late”

- **Description (optional):**  
  “Short daily newsletter with links, summaries, and takeaways.”

- **Visual Notes:**  
  - Show a section of the email that specifically calls out tools or links (with real or realistic examples).  
  - Optionally overlay 2–3 “card” elements representing different tools, each with a short label.  
  - Avoid fake logos; keep it obviously editorial, not product ads.

## Angle 4 – Noise vs Signal (“Escape the Doom-Scroll”)

**Pillar:** Curated Signal / Time Reclaimed  
**Use for:** Broad audiences + retargeting.

### Static Ad: `static_noise-vs-signal_mar26_v1`

- **Primary Text (Prospecting)**  
  “Your feeds are noise. TLDR filters the world of tech into one skimmable email — so you can stop doom‑scrolling and still know what’s going on.”

- **Primary Text (Retargeting)**  
  “If you’re tired of scrolling past the same takes, TLDR is the one email that only shows up when it has something useful.”

- **Headline Options:**
  1. “Escape the Scroll, Keep the Signal”
  2. “From Infinite Scroll to One Email”
  3. “Less Noise. More Signal.”

- **Description (optional):**  
  “A single, curated tech briefing. Free in your inbox.”

- **Visual Notes:**  
  - Split-screen: chaotic social feed on one side, clean TLDR email on the other.  
  - Or simple “feed” blurred behind a crisp newsletter screenshot.  
  - Use subtle motion for video versions (scrolling, swipe transition from noise to TLDR).

## Video / Reels Concepts (9:16)

These can be adapted for UGC, founder‑led, or motion‑graphics style.

### Video 1: “Morning Routine” – `video_morning-routine_mar26_v1`

- **Hook (first 2 seconds):**  
  On‑screen text: “How I keep up with tech in 5 minutes.”  
  Visual: Person opening inbox on phone; TLDR email at the top.

- **Script (15s version):**  
  1. “There’s no way I’m reading 10 different tech sites every morning.”  
  2. “So I let TLDR do the work — one email with the important stories, tools, and weird internet stuff worth knowing.”  
  3. “Takes about five minutes to skim, and I’m caught up for the day.”  

- **CTA line:**  
  “If you work in tech (or just care about it), subscribe to TLDR. It’s free.”

### Video 2: “What You Get” – `video_what-you-get_mar26_v1`

- **Hook:**  
  On‑screen text: “This is what TLDR looks like.”  
  Quick scroll through a real TLDR issue.

- **Script (voiceover or captions):**  
  1. “Top stories with short, honest summaries.”  
  2. “Hand‑picked links to tools, experiments, and ideas.”  
  3. “A few fun things at the end so it doesn’t feel like homework.”  
  4. “It’s the email I wish I had years ago.”  

- **CTA line:**  
  “Pop your email in and you’ll get the next issue.”
