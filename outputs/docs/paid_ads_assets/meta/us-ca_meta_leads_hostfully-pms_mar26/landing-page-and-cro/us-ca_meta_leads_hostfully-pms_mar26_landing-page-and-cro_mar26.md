# Landing Page and CRO Handoff - us-ca_meta_leads_hostfully-pms_mar26 (Mar 2026)

## Landing Page Goal

Convert cold Meta traffic into qualified demo requests for Hostfully PMS in US/CA.

## Required Above-the-Fold Elements

- Headline aligned to ad angle (operations clarity / automation / direct growth)
- Subhead with who this is for (vacation rental owner-operators and PM teams)
- Primary CTA: `Book Demo`
- Trust signal row (logos, ratings, or short proof bullets)

## Form UX Requirements

- Keep initial form to 4-6 fields max
- Include portfolio-size selector (`1-4`, `5-15`, `16-30`, `31+`)
- Ask booking software status to pre-qualify intent
- Confirmation step should trigger thank-you event + meeting CTA

## Message Match Rules

- **Angle 1 ads** land on operational unification hero.
- **Angle 2 ads** land on automation workflow proof.
- **Angle 3 ads** land on direct booking/revenue control proof.
- **Angle 4 ads** land on guest communication consistency proof.

## CRO Test Backlog

1. CTA copy: `Book Demo` vs `See Hostfully PMS`
2. Hero framing: pain-first vs outcome-first
3. Social proof placement: above vs below form
4. Form length: short vs enriched qualification fields
