# Creative Brief - meta-connected-ops-v1_mar26

## Concept

Hostfully PMS as the single connected operating system for vacation rental operators.

## Audience

B2B owner-operators and small PM teams in US/CA.

## Message

Stop managing operations across fragmented tools. Centralize bookings, channels, and communication in one platform.

## Deliverables

- 1x static 1:1
- 1x static 4:5
- 1x 15-30s video cut (optional)

## Copy Direction

- Headline: "Run Your Rental Ops in One Place"
- CTA: "Book Demo"
- Tone: practical, operator-first, no hype
