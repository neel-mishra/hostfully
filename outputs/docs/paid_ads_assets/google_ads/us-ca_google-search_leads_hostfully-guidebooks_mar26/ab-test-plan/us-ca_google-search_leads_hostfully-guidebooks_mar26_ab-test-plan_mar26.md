# AB Test Plan – us-ca_google-search_leads_hostfully-guidebooks_mar26

## Test 1: Differentiator Priority

- Variant A: Revenue-focused lead message.
- Variant B: Fast setup and scale-focused lead message.
- Variant C: Guest experience and support load reduction message.
- Success metric: lowest CPA at acceptable conversion volume.

## Test 2: CTA Framing

- Variant A: Book a FREE demo.
- Variant B: See guidebook examples then book demo.
- Success metric: conversion rate lift without CPA increase.

## Test 3: Qualification Intent

- Variant A: Host-focused language.
- Variant B: Property manager-focused language.
- Success metric: downstream lead quality from CRM feedback.

## Test Cadence

- Week 1: Validate tracking, keep baseline stable.
- Week 2: Launch Test 1.
- Week 3: Launch Test 2 if traffic threshold is met.
- Week 4: Launch Test 3 and reallocate to best winner.

## Decision Rules

- Use minimum 2-week windows where possible.
- Require directional consistency across CPA and CVR.
- Pause clear underperformers quickly if spend is inefficient.
