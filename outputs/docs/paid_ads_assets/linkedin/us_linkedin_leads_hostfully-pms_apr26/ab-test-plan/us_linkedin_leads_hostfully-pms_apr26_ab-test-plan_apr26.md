## 1. Experiment Framework

- **Primary KPI:** Cost per qualified demo request
- **Secondary KPIs:** CTR, landing page conversion rate, MQL rate, SQL rate

## Test Sequence (April)

1. **Message angle test (Week 1-2)**
   - Operational Control vs Revenue Efficiency vs Team Throughput
   - Success criteria: >= 15% better qualified demo CPL than control
2. **CTA test (Week 2-3)**
   - Book Demo vs Learn More
   - Success criteria: higher conversion rate with stable lead quality
3. **Audience depth test (Week 3-4)**
   - Role-focused targeting vs broader skills/interests
   - Success criteria: maintain MQL rate while expanding volume

## Guardrails

- Keep daily budget changes <= 20% while tests are active.
- Avoid changing creative and audience simultaneously in same ad group.
- Require minimum sample threshold before pausing variants.
