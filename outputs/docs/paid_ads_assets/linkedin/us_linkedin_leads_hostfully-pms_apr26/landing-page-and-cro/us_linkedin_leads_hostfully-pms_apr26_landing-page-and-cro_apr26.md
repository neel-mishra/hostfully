## Funnel Definition

- **Traffic source:** linkedin
- **Offer:** PMS demo for short-term rental operators and property managers
- **Core promise:** Streamline portfolio operations and improve team throughput with one PMS

## Recommended Landing Page Structure

1. **Hero:** Primary value prop + "Book Demo" CTA
2. **Pain points:** Fragmented tooling, manual workflows, inconsistent operations
3. **Outcome mapping:** Feature clusters tied to operational and revenue outcomes
4. **Proof:** Customer logos, short case snippets, metric-backed claims
5. **How it works:** 3-step implementation path
6. **FAQ:** Integrations, onboarding effort, migration concerns
7. **Bottom CTA:** Demo request form with minimal fields

## CRO Hypotheses

- **H1:** Outcome-led hero headline improves conversion rate vs product-feature-led headline.
- **H2:** Adding implementation-time clarity ("live in weeks") lifts form completion.
- **H3:** Short form (name/email/company/portfolio size) outperforms long qualification form.
