## 1. Experiment Framework

- **Primary KPI:** Cost per qualified sponsor meeting (SQL) from LinkedIn.
- **Secondary KPIs:** Cost per sponsor lead, CTR, landing-page conversion rate.

## 2. Test 1 – Creative Angle Test (Prospecting)

Compare the 4 core angles (Outperform Paid Social, Audience Concentration, Case Study, Calendar Scarcity) across Demand Gen and Marketing Leader campaigns. Keep targeting and bidding constant; rotate 3–4 ads per campaign and evaluate CPA + CTR after 1–2 weeks.

## 3. Test 2 – Audience Mix

Once winners emerge from Test 1, compare:

- Demand Gen / Growth-only targeting.
- Marketing Leaders-only targeting.
- Mixed targeting including both segments.

Measure CPA and meeting quality. Shift budget toward best-performing audience mix.
