## Core Angles & Example Ads

### Angle 1 – Outperform Paid Social (vs LinkedIn / Meta)

**Promise:** “Stop paying for impressions, pay for attention.”

- **Primary text (Single Image Ad):**  
  “You already pay LinkedIn and Meta to reach ‘B2B decision-makers’. TLDR readers are the ones actually shipping the products and buying the tools. Sponsors see consistent pipeline from one focused newsletter buy.”

- **Headline options:**
  1. “Outperform Your Paid Social”
  2. “Turn Ad Budget into Real Pipeline”

- **CTA button:** “Book a demo”

**Use in campaigns:**  
Campaign A (Demand Gen) + Campaign B (Marketing Leaders).

---

### Angle 2 – Audience Concentration & Signal

**Promise:** “All your best-fit buyers in one inbox.”

- **Primary text:**  
  “Instead of chasing your ICP across feeds, TLDR puts your message in one place they already check every morning. Sponsors reach a concentrated audience of engineers, founders, and product leaders with almost zero noise.”

- **Headline options:**
  1. “Your Buyers All Read the Same Email”
  2. “Reach Builders, Not Just Browsers”

- **CTA button:** “Get the sponsor kit”

**Use in campaigns:**  
All three campaigns; especially ABM.

---

### Angle 3 – Case Study / Proof

**Promise:** “Sponsors close real deals.”

- **Primary text (swap in your real numbers later):**  
  “A B2B SaaS sponsor booked XX meetings and closed YY in new ARR from a single TLDR placement. When your message lands in a curated, trusted newsletter, readers actually take action.”

- **Headline options:**
  1. “From Newsletter Slot to Signed Deals”
  2. “Sponsors See Real Pipeline, Not Vanity Metrics”

- **CTA button:** “See example results”

**Use in campaigns:**  
Marketing Leaders + ABM; especially in retargeting or warm audiences once you build them.

---

### Angle 4 – Calendar & Scarcity

**Promise:** “Limited inventory, plan ahead.”

- **Primary text:**  
  “TLDR sells out sponsor inventory months in advance. If you want your launches, events, or big pushes in front of our readers, you can’t wait for the week-of. Plan your next quarter’s campaigns now.”

- **Headline options:**
  1. “Reserve Your Slot Before It’s Gone”
  2. “Sponsor Calendar Fills Up Fast”

- **CTA button:** “Check availability”

**Use in campaigns:**  
All campaigns, but especially for high-intent visitors and ABM.

## Format Recommendations

- **Primary formats:**
  - Single Image Ads: sponsor message + TLDR brand.
  - Document Ads: 1–2 page sponsor kit or case study (“How sponsors use TLDR to reach builders”).

- **Optional formats:**
  - Video Ad: 15–30s explainer of TLDR audience + sponsor outcomes.

Map each angle to:

- 1–2 Single Image Ads (different visuals).
- 1 Document Ad (case study / sponsor kit).
