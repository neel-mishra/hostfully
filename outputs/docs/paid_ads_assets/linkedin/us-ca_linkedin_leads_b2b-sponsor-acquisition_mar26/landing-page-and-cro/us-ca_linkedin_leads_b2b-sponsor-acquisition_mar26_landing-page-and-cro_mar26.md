## Funnel Definition

- **Traffic source:** LinkedIn sponsor campaigns (Demand Gen, Marketing Leaders, ABM).
- **Offer:** “Sponsor TLDR” – newsletter sponsorships and integrated campaigns.
- **Core promise:** “Reach a concentrated audience of builders and tech decision-makers more efficiently than paid social.”

**Baseline funnel:**

1. Ad click → `/advertise` or dedicated sponsor landing page.
2. Visitor reads hero + value props above the fold.
3. Visitor submits short form to request sponsor kit / intro call.
4. Visitor lands on thank-you page with next steps.

## Recommended Landing Page Structure

### Above the fold

- **Hero headline:**  
  “Turn one newsletter placement into real pipeline.”

- **Subhead:**  
  “TLDR reaches a concentrated audience of builders, engineers, and tech leaders who actually buy tools—not just scroll past ads.”

- **Primary CTA/form:**  
  - Short form (Name, Company, Role, Email, Budget range).  
  - Button: “Get the sponsor kit” or “Book a sponsor intro”.

- **Visual:**  
  - TLDR email preview + sponsor slot highlighted, or logos of representative sponsors.

### Social proof & credibility

- Past or current sponsors (logos, if allowed).
- Short bullets:
  - “High-intent, opt-in audience.”
  - “Performance that rivals your paid social.”
  - “Inventory sells out months in advance.”

### What you get

- Section outlining:
  - Newsletter placements (+ specs).
  - Optional bundles (multi-issue runs, integrated packages).
  - Reporting and measurement (clicks, leads, meetings).

### FAQ / objections

- “Is this right for our ACV and deal size?”
- “What kind of results do sponsors see?”
- “How far in advance do we need to book?”
- “How do you handle tracking and attribution?”
