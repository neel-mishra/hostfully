## 1. Prerequisites

- LinkedIn Campaign Manager access and billing set up.
- LinkedIn Insight Tag installed on:
  - Sponsor landing page.
  - Thank-you / confirmation page.
- Custom conversion defined for “Sponsor lead submitted”.

## 2. Campaign Group & Campaigns

1. Create or open the **Campaign Group**: `us_linkedin_sponsors_tldr_mar26`.
2. Inside it, create three campaigns:
   - `us_linkedin_leads_sponsors_demand-gen_mar26`
   - `us_linkedin_leads_sponsors_marketing-leaders_mar26`
   - `us_linkedin_leads_sponsors_abm-target-accounts_mar26`

Set objective to **Website Conversions** and daily budgets in line with the structure doc.
