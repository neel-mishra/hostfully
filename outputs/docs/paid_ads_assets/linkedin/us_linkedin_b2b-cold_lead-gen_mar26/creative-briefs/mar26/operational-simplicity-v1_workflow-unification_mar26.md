## 4. Safe Zones & Layout Grid

### Safe Zone Measurements (all sizes)

| Size | Dimensions | Safe Content Zone | Top | Bottom | Left/Right | Overlay Elements |
|------|------------|-------------------|-----|--------|------------|------------------|
| LinkedIn Square | 1080 x 1080 | 960 x 900 | 90 px | 90 px | 60 px | UI crop risk in feed previews |
| LinkedIn Landscape | 1200 x 628 | 1080 x 500 | 64 px | 64 px | 60 px | CTA and metadata below image |

### Recommended Layout

- **Logo zone:** top-right, inside safe margin
- **Headline zone:** upper-left within safe zone
- **Stat/number zone:** center-right (optional)
- **CTA zone:** lower-left above bottom safe margin
- **Background focus:** left side "chaos", right side "clarity"

### Designer Notes

- Keep visual style enterprise-clean, not startup-neon.
- Use moderate contrast to preserve legibility in LinkedIn dark/light UI contexts.
- Avoid dense icon grids; prioritize one dominant comparison motif.
