## 2. Concept Overview

**Concept Name:** Pipeline Clarity

**Description:** Visualize a transition from vanity metrics (clicks/impressions) to pipeline metrics (MQL, SQL, opportunities). Use a clear funnel/progression graphic with restrained enterprise styling.

**Visual Hook:** A single bold line moving from "Clicks" to "Pipeline."

**On-Image Copy:**

## 4. Safe Zones & Layout Grid

### Safe Zone Measurements (all sizes)

| Size | Dimensions | Safe Content Zone | Top | Bottom | Left/Right | Overlay Elements |
|------|------------|-------------------|-----|--------|------------|------------------|
| LinkedIn Square | 1080 x 1080 | 960 x 900 | 90 px | 90 px | 60 px | Feed crop risk on smaller screens |
| LinkedIn Landscape | 1200 x 628 | 1080 x 500 | 64 px | 64 px | 60 px | Metadata strip under creative |

### Recommended Layout

- **Logo zone:** top-left, compact lockup
- **Headline zone:** upper-middle
- **Stat/number zone:** center with high contrast
- **CTA zone:** lower-right in safe area
- **Background focus:** simple gradient + line/funnel motif

### Designer Notes

- Keep numbers believable and easy to swap once proof points are approved.
- Prioritize legibility at small feed preview sizes.
- Avoid finance-dashboard clutter; one core chart motif only.
