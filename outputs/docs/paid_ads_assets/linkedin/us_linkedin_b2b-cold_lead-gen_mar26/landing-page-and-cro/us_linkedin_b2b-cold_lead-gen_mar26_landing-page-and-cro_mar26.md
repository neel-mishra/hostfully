## Recommended Landing Page Structure

1. **Hero:** Clear value prop + primary CTA ("Book a Demo")
2. **Pain-to-solution section:** Current fragmentation vs integrated workflow
3. **Proof section:** Customer logos, quantified outcomes, case snapshots
4. **Feature-to-outcome mapping:** What capabilities produce what business results
5. **FAQ and objection handling:** Implementation effort, integrations, time-to-value
6. **Bottom CTA:** Repeat conversion prompt with trust signals

- **H3:** Short form (4 fields) lifts conversion volume vs long form (8+ fields).
