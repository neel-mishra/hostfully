# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Rapid cuts of people scrolling through emails, opening newsletters, then immediately cutting to a vibrant, active online community (Discord, Slack, forums). Maybe some firework animations for emphasis.
- **Audio**: (High-energy music intro) "Newsletters are DYING!" *beat drop*

## Body (Main Content)
- [Scene 1]: [Visual / B-roll] Person looking bored scrolling a long email.] | [Narration]: "Broadcasting to an audience? SO 2023. They just consume."
- [Scene 2]: [Visual] Fast zoom into a vibrant online community, people chatting, emojis flying, animated comments.] | [Narration]: "2026 is all about COMMUNITY HUBS! Where subscribers talk to *each other*."
- [Scene 3]: [Visual] Animated graphics with these keywords: "Stickiness," "Emotional Moat," "Interaction," each highlighted in a different color.] | [Narration]: "Why? Peer support creates stickiness content can't touch. Inside jokes build an emotional MOAT... and anticipation moves to the INTERACTION!"
- [Scene 4]: [Visual] Image of a megaphone being smashed with a cartoon hammer.  Then quickly transition to a warm, inviting campfire with people gathered around. | [Narration]: "Stop building a MEGAPHONE... start building a CAMPFIRE!"

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Build a Community! Learn How Below!" Arrow pointing down to the caption.
- **Audio**: (Music fades slightly) "Tap that button for game-changing strategies!" (Quick, upbeat music outro)
