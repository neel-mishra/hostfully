# Video Script

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Fast cuts of someone hitting the "SPAM" button on emails repeatedly with increasing frustration. Dramatic zoom on the "SPAM" button.
- **Audio**: (Loud, frustrated sigh, followed by assertive voice) "STOP IT! Just STOP!"

## Body (Main Content)
- **[Scene 1]:** [Visual / B-roll: Quick cuts of overly enthusiastic email subject lines flashing across the screen: "RE:," "Urgent!," tons of emojis, all caps words. Each gets a red X over it.] | [Narration: "2024, and you're STILL using clickbait subject lines? You're HURTING your brand!"]
- **[Scene 2]:** [Visual: Person looking exasperated, shaking their head. Then transitions to split screen: ON TOP - Spam folder overflowing with emails. BELOW - Clean inbox with professional, straightforward emails.] | [Narration: "Deceptive subject lines kill trust! Excessive emojis trigger spam filters! ALL CAPS is screaming 'delete me!"]
- **[Scene 3]:** [Visual: Show a clean, concise email on a phone screen: "Weekly Marketing Report - Q3 Results".  Person looking directly at the camera, speaking calmly and confidently.] | [Narration: "Be boring! The best subject lines are CLEAR, concise, and deliver value upfront. Under 50 characters!"]

## Call to Action
- **Visual**: [Text overlay: "Ditch the Clickbait! Be Clear!"]
- **Audio**: [CTA: "Follow for more email marketing secrets! #emailmarketing #marketingtips #copywriting"]
