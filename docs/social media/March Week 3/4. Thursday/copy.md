## LinkedIn Post Copy

The 2026 Newsletter Checklist (Save this for your next send):

1. Subject Line Clarity: Short dominates. Aim for scannable and descriptive over 'clever.'
2. The Morning Peak: Schedule sends for the early morning window to catch the pre-work routine.
3. The Weekly Cadence: For local or niche hubs, weekly is the sweet spot for sustainability and anticipation.
4. Clear Purpose: Does your community solve a specific problem? If you can't name it, they won't join.
5. Active Moderation: Set the culture early. Proactive culture-setting beats reactive moderation every time.

Which of these are you missing?
