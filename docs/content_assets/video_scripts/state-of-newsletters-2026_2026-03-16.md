# Video Script: The State of Newsletters in 2026

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
**Source:** docs/blogs/2026-03-16/Read-the-State-of-Newsletters-report.md

---

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual:** Bold animated number: "28,000,000,000" with envelope icons flying
- **Audio:** "28 billion emails. 255 million readers. And open rates just keep climbing."

## Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual:** Side-by-side chart — social media reach declining, email open rates rising
- **Audio:** "The 2026 State of Newsletters report is out. Email is officially the most predictable distribution channel in media. No algorithm decides who sees your content."

## Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual:** Countdown animation: "6 months → 66 days" with calendar graphic
- **Audio:** "The biggest shift: time to first dollar. New newsletters launched last year hit revenue in a median of 66 days. Two years ago that was six months. The infrastructure caught up."

## Scene 3 (25-40s)
- **Visual:** Text overlays with each stat popping up
- **Audio:** "Three things that matter in the data. Open rates clearing 40% — TLDR's flagship hits 48% across 1.6 million subscribers. Niche beats broad — 50,000 DevOps engineers is worth more than 500,000 'tech enthusiasts.' And newsletter ads deliver 50% lower CPC than LinkedIn."

## Scene 4 (40-50s)
- **Visual:** Clock showing 6-8am, calendar highlighting Tuesday-Wednesday
- **Audio:** "The tactical stuff: send before 8am local time, Tuesday or Wednesday. Test it for four weeks. Most publishers never test send times and leave 5-10% open rate improvement on the table."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual:** Text overlay: "Full data breakdown on the blog" + TLDR logo
- **Audio:** "The email list is the asset. Everything else — podcasts, events, courses — is a monetization layer on top. Full report breakdown linked in bio."
