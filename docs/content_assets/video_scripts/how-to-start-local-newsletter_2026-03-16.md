# Video Script: How To Start a Newsletter for Local Communities

**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)
**Source:** docs/blogs/2026-03-16/How-To-Start-a-Newsletter-for-Local-Communities.md

---

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual:** Bold text overlay: "Local news is DYING" with newspaper layoffs montage
- **Audio:** "Newsrooms have cut 60% of their staff since 2005. Here's what's replacing them."

## Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual:** Split screen — empty newsroom desk vs. someone reading a newsletter on their phone
- **Audio:** "Newsletters. Specifically, local ones. You own the list, you control distribution, and here's the thing — nobody else is covering your neighborhood."

## Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual:** Text overlay animating each rule as it's said
- **Audio:** "Three rules. One: pick a community, not a city. 'Downtown Austin parents' is a newsletter. 'Austin news' is a newspaper. Two: curate five things, not fifty. Three: send weekly. Consistency beats frequency."

## Scene 3 (25-40s)
- **Visual:** B-roll of farmers market, coffee shop counter with QR code, person forwarding email
- **Audio:** "Growth is different for local newsletters. Forget going viral. Partner with local businesses. Put QR codes at coffee shops. Write 'Best Parks in Your City' content that ranks in Google. Word of mouth is the engine."

## Scene 4 (40-50s)
- **Visual:** Simple calculator animation showing 2,000 subscribers x $100/week sponsor
- **Audio:** "You don't need 100,000 subscribers. Two thousand engaged readers plus one local sponsor per issue — that's a real business. Restaurants, real estate agents, service providers — they all want to reach your neighborhood."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual:** Text overlay: "Full playbook on the blog" + TLDR logo
- **Audio:** "The hard part isn't strategy. It's showing up every single week. Full tactical playbook linked in bio."
