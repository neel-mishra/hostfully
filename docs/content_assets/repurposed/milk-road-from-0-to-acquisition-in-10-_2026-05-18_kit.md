## Executive Summary

Shaan Puri and Ben Levy launched Milk Road in early 2022 — a free daily crypto newsletter — and sold it for a reported $5M+ just 10 months later, with 250,000 subscribers on the list at the time of acquisition.

**Format and positioning.** Milk Road's core bet was simplicity: one daily email summarizing crypto news in five minutes, written in conversational English. The niche was crowded but underserved. Most crypto media either assumed deep financial literacy or offered no editorial quality control. Milk Road occupied the middle — accessible but not dumbed down.

**Infrastructure decision.** The team chose beehiiv over building custom tooling. This compressed their launch timeline from months to days and gave them access to a built-in cross-promotion network with other crypto newsletters on the platform. The infrastructure decision directly accelerated subscriber acquisition.

**Growth mechanics.** A referral program rewarded readers with branded merchandise — t-shirts, hats, stickers — which doubled as both incentive and organic advertising. beehiiv automated tracking and fulfillment. The resulting flywheel linked content quality to open rates, open rates to referrals, referrals to subscriber count, and subscriber count to advertising demand.

**Revenue model.** Milk Road ran on 100% advertising revenue with no paywall. Three ad tiers per issue, all copy written in-house to match the newsletter's editorial voice. Open rates hit 40-48%, roughly 2-3x the industry average of 15-20%, which drove premium ad pricing and strong renewal rates.

**Acquisition rationale.** At 250K engaged subscribers with proven open rates and a repeatable ad model, Milk Road presented a clean acquisition target. The roughly $20-per-subscriber price was high but defensible given audience engagement, niche value (crypto-native readers with high purchase intent), and operational simplicity (two-person team, minimal overhead).

**Key takeaway for operators.** Nothing in the Milk Road playbook was novel. The advantage was speed, niche selection, and disciplined focus. Five decisions drove the outcome: choosing a niche with daily urgency, using a platform over custom infrastructure, leveraging merch for referrals, avoiding paywall friction, and timing the exit to peak engagement.
