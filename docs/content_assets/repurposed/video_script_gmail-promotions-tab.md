# Video Script: Gmail's Promotions Tab Isn't Your Enemy

**Source:** Why-Your-Emails-Are-Going-to-Gmails-Promotions.md
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (TikTok/Reels)

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Bold on-screen text: "48% OPEN RATE — STILL IN PROMOTIONS" over a quick cut of Gmail tabs (Primary / Promotions). Finger taps Promotions.
- **Audio**: "Forty-eight percent opens — Promotions tab anyway."

## Body (5-45s)

### Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Simple checklist animates: bulk send, schedule, same template, commercial tone — all checkmarks. Caption: "NEWSLETTER = PROMOTIONS BY DESIGN"
- **Narration**: Newsletters hit every signal Gmail uses for Promotions: list sends, fixed schedules, consistent templates. Morning Brew and The Hustle live there too. It is not a quality score.

### Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Split screen: "NOT SPAM" (inbox, visible) vs "SPAM" (buried). Then text: "READER BEHAVIOR > SUBJECT LINES"
- **Narration**: Promotions means delivered and filed, not hidden. Spam is a totally different problem. Tab placement follows what each reader does, not your subject line hacks.

### Scene 3 (25-35s)
- **Visual**: Quick icons: open mail, reply arrow, drag between tabs, contact card. Caption: "TEACH GMAIL"
- **Narration**: Opens, replies, dragging to Primary, and adding the sender to contacts train Gmail for that person. Engagement drops or weird send spikes can flip you back — fix the relationship, not the tab.

### Scene 4 (35-45s)
- **Visual**: Creator at desk, calm. On-screen: "CONSISTENCY · TRUST · LIST HYGIENE · SPF/DKIM/DMARC"
- **Narration**: Stop gaming the classifier. Send on a rhythm people expect, deliver what the subject line promises, cut long-term non-openers, and keep authentication clean so you stay out of Spam.

## Call to Action (45-60s)
- **Visual**: End card: logo or handle, text "FULL BREAKDOWN — LINK IN BIO" (or your site URL)
- **Audio**: "We broke down how Gmail actually sorts mail and what to do next — link in bio."

## Notes
- **Tone**: Direct, slightly contrarian, no panic — same energy as explaining a system instead of selling a hack.
- **Music**: Light indie electronic or lo-fi, mid-tempo, not dramatic — keeps it smart-casual.
- **Key stat for text overlay**: 48% open rate (TLDR flagship — Promotions is not a death sentence)
