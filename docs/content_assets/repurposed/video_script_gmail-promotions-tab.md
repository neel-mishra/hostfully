# Video Script: Why Your Emails Land in Gmail Promotions

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Split screen — left side shows email in Promotions tab, right side shows 48% open rate dashboard
- **Audio**: "Your newsletter is in Gmail Promotions. Here's why that doesn't matter."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Gmail inbox with tabs highlighted, zoom into Promotions tab
- **Audio**: "Gmail sorts newsletters into Promotions because they're bulk email. Scheduled delivery, templated HTML, large send lists — Gmail sees the pattern and routes accordingly. This is not Spam. It's organization."

### Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Side-by-side comparison graphic — Promotions (checkmark, "delivered, searchable, visible") vs. Spam (X mark, "buried, damages reputation")
- **Audio**: "Emails in Promotions are fully delivered and searchable. Emails in Spam damage your sender reputation. Stop conflating the two."

### Scene 3 (25-40s)
- **Visual**: Bar chart showing open rates — "Industry avg: 15-20%" vs "Top newsletters: 40%+" with text overlay "Same tab. Different results."
- **Audio**: "The newsletters with 40% open rates aren't gaming tab placement. They're building habits. Trust through consistency. Value in every issue. Readers who actively seek them out."

### Scene 4 (40-50s)
- **Visual**: Three text cards appearing one at a time: "TRUST" → "HABIT" → "VALUE DENSITY"
- **Audio**: "Three things drive open rates. Trust — show up consistently. Habit — same time every day. Value density — every issue earns its place. None of these require Primary tab placement."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Stop optimizing for inbox tab. Start optimizing for 'worth finding.'" with link
- **Audio**: "The Promotions tab is a classification, not a verdict. Full breakdown linked below."
