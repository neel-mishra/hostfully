# Video Script: Why Your Emails Land in Gmail Promotions

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Close-up of phone screen — Gmail Promotions tab opens, revealing dozens of unread marketing emails. Text overlay smashes in: "THIS ISN'T SPAM."
- **Audio**: "Your emails aren't being punished. Gmail's just organizing them. And that's fine."

## Body (Main Content)

- **[Scene 1 — 5-15s]**: Screen recording of Gmail tabs (Primary, Social, Promotions) with arrows showing email sorting. Simple animation: email icon slides from inbox into Promotions folder.
  | "Gmail's Promotions tab isn't a penalty. It's a filing cabinet. Over a billion users browse it on purpose. Your emails are delivered — they're just categorized."

- **[Scene 2 — 15-28s]**: Bold stat card: "48% OPEN RATE" zooms in, followed by "vs. industry average: 15-20%." Cut to TLDR logo and subscriber count (7M+) appearing on screen.
  | "TLDR sends to millions of Gmail inboxes every weekday. Our open rate is 48% — more than double the industry average. Promotions placement hasn't stopped that. So what actually matters?"

- **[Scene 3 — 28-42s]**: Quick-cut list appearing one line at a time — each point flies in with a whoosh sound effect. Icons for each: speech bubble (reply), contact card, drag-and-drop arrow.
  | "Three things move the needle. One — tell subscribers to drag your email to Primary. Gmail asks to confirm, then it sticks. Two — get them to reply, even once. Three — add your sending address to contacts. Put all three in your welcome email."

- **[Scene 4 — 42-52s]**: Split screen: left side shows a heavy HTML email template (red X over it), right side shows a clean plain-text email (green checkmark). Text overlay: "Write like a person, not a billboard."
  | "On the sending side: ditch the heavy HTML templates. Limit your links. Skip the 'EXCLUSIVE DEAL' language. Gmail's classifier reads those signals. Clean emails look personal. Personal emails get opened."

## Call to Action (52-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay fades in: "Promotions ≠ Spam. Consistency = Deliverability." followed by "Follow for more email strategy."
- **Audio**: "Promotions isn't a crisis. Spam is a crisis. If subscribers trust you enough to open — and you show up consistently — the tab doesn't matter. Follow for more."

## Script Notes
- Target length: 60 seconds
- Platform: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- Tone: TLDR voice — concise, authoritative, slightly wry. Not salesy.
- Key stat to feature: 48% open rates despite Promotions tab
- Pacing: Fast cuts, no dead air. Each scene transition uses a hard cut or swipe.
- Music: Uptempo lo-fi or electronic — nothing distracting, stays under narration.
- Captions: Burned-in bold captions throughout (accessibility + silent autoplay).
