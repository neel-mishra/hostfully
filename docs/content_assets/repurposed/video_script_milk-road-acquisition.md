# Video Script: Milk Road — 0 to Acquired in 10 Months

## Hook (0–5s)
- **Visual**: Bold white text on black: "$5M FOR A NEWSLETTER." Smash cut to a phone screen scrolling a Milk Road email. Quick zoom effect.
- **Audio**: "Two guys built a newsletter to 250,000 subscribers and sold it for five million dollars. It took them ten months."

## Body (Main Content)

- **[Scene 1 — The Bet] (5–15s)**: Split-screen showing crypto Twitter chaos on one side, a clean Milk Road email on the other. Text overlay: "Early 2022."
  | "Shaan Puri and Ben Levy saw the gap. Crypto was everywhere, but most coverage was either PhD-level or straight-up incoherent. Their pitch: five minutes, plain English, every morning. One email. That's the whole product."

- **[Scene 2 — The Flywheel] (15–30s)**: Motion graphic of a spinning flywheel — subscribers → opens → referrals → ads → revenue → growth. Quick cuts of merch photos (hats, tees).
  | "They launched on beehiiv — no engineering team, no custom stack. Then they turned readers into recruiters. Refer a friend, get Milk Road merch. Crypto people love signaling tribe membership. Every hoodie was a walking billboard. That referral loop plus beehiiv's recommendation engine made growth compound on itself."

- **[Scene 3 — The Numbers] (30–45s)**: Key stats flying in one by one as bold text overlays. 250K subs. 40%+ open rates. $0 paywall. 100% ad-supported.
  | "Two hundred fifty thousand subscribers. Open rates above 40 percent — the industry average is fifteen to twenty. Revenue was all advertising. Crypto exchanges and DeFi protocols paid a premium to reach that audience. Milk Road wrote the ad copy themselves so it matched the editorial voice. Readers barely noticed the difference."

- **[Scene 4 — The Exit] (45–55s)**: Calculator graphic showing $5M ÷ 250K = $20/subscriber. Transition to a timeline graphic: Jan 2022 → Oct 2022, with "ACQUIRED" stamp.
  | "Ten months in, they sold. Roughly twenty dollars per subscriber. High — but the engagement backed it up. They timed it at peak subscriber count in a crypto bull market. That wasn't luck. That was the plan."

## Call to Action (55–60s)
- **Visual**: TLDR logo. Text overlay: "Follow for more breakdowns like this."
- **Audio**: "The playbook isn't complicated. The execution is. Follow for more."

---

## Script Notes
- **Target length**: 60 seconds
- **Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Tone**: TLDR voice — concise, authoritative, slightly wry. Not salesy.
- **Pacing**: Fast cuts every 3–5 seconds. Text overlays reinforce key numbers. No dead air.
- **Key stats**: 250K subscribers in 10 months, ~$5M acquisition, $20/subscriber, 40%+ open rates
- **Music**: Lo-fi beat or trending audio with energy — not hype-house, not corporate. Think "smart casual."
- **On-screen text style**: Bold sans-serif, high contrast, minimal words per frame. Stats get their own frames.
- **Source blog**: Milk Road: 0 to 250K Subscribers to $5M+ Acquisition in 10 Months
