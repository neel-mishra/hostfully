# Video Script: Milk Road — DM to Acquisition in 10 Months

**Source:** Milk-Road-From-0-to-Acquisition-in-10-months.md
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Format:** Vertical (TikTok/Reels)

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline graphic: "0" to "250K+" with "10 MONTHS" stamped across. Quick flash: "ACQUIRED"
- **Audio**: "Quarter million subscribers in ten months."

## Body (5-45s)

### Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Twitter DM bubble mockup, then "NO LIST · NO ARCHIVE · NO BRAND" crossed out one by one as "DAILY CRYPTO NEWSLETTER" appears.
- **Narration**: Milk Road started as a DM between Shaan Puri and Ben Levy: no list, no back catalog, no brand. They bet a short, fun daily crypto read could cut through a noisy market.

### Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual**: "1 REFERRAL = FREE PDF" with a simple reward graphic; automation icon (no manual grind).
- **Narration**: Referrals that actually worked: one referral unlocked a free investor PDF, automated end to end. Readers became the distribution channel.

### Scene 3 (25-35s)
- **Visual**: Montage: meme-style ad frame, handshake "NEWSLETTER SWAP", no-code blocks stacking "LANDING · FORMS · SITE"
- **Narration**: They ran meme-y paid ads on Facebook and TikTok and killed what was expensive. Cross-promoted with other newsletters. No-code stack so they could ship tests without waiting on engineers.

### Scene 4 (35-45s)
- **Visual**: Poll UI in an email; text: "CONTENT + DISTRIBUTION"
- **Narration**: Polls in every issue kept people interacting and showed what to cover next. The lesson is not secret tactics — it is good enough content plus a growth system that compounds fast.

## Call to Action (45-60s)
- **Visual**: "THE FIVE-LEVER PLAYBOOK — LINK IN BIO" with brand handle
- **Audio**: "We unpacked all five growth levers — link in bio."

## Notes
- **Tone**: Fast, story-driven, slightly incredulous at the timeline — informed, not hypey.
- **Music**: Tight modern beat, subtle crypto-adjacent energy without cliché "blockchain" drops.
- **Key stat for text overlay**: 250K+ subscribers in 10 months (then acquired)
