# Video Script: Milk Road — 0 to Acquisition in 10 Months

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Timeline animation — "Day 0: Zero subscribers" morphing to "Month 10: 250K+ subscribers. Acquired."
- **Audio**: "Zero subscribers to acquired in ten months. Here's the exact playbook."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Two decision cards appearing: "No custom tech" and "Growth from day one"
- **Audio**: "Two decisions shaped everything. First: no custom infrastructure. No-code platform, built-in growth tools. Zero engineering time meant 100% focus on content and distribution. Second: the referral program shipped with the first issue. Not the fiftieth."

### Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Referral flow diagram — subscriber shares link → friend signs up → reward delivered automatically
- **Audio**: "The referral program was simple. One reward — a crypto investor PDF. One action — share your link. Fully automated. Your earliest readers are your most enthusiastic sharers. Give them tools before the enthusiasm fades."

### Scene 3 (25-40s)
- **Visual**: Five channel icons appearing in a circle: "Organic" + "Referrals" + "Cross-promos" + "Paid ads" + "Content" connecting to center "250K+"
- **Audio**: "No single channel carried the business. Organic discovery. Referrals turning readers into recruiters. Cross-promotions borrowing trust. Paid acquisition — tracked by 30-day retention, not just signups. And daily content designed for five-minute consumption."

### Scene 4 (40-50s)
- **Visual**: Split screen — left: "Cost per acquisition" crossed out. Right: "Quality per acquisition" highlighted. Below: "Memes > brand ads"
- **Audio**: "Most operators measure cost per acquisition. Milk Road measured quality per acquisition. Which ad creatives attracted subscribers who actually stuck around? Memes and social proof outperformed polished brand ads."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "The fastest-growing newsletters execute the obvious tactics with discipline and speed." with link
- **Audio**: "No secret tactics. Just referrals, partnerships, paid with feedback loops, and great content — executed fast. Full case study linked below."
