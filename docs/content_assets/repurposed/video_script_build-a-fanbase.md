# Video Script: How To Build a Fanbase — Followers vs. True Fans

## Hook (0-5s)
- **Visual**: Counter animation — "10,000 subscribers" crossed out, replaced by "1,000 true fans" with fire emoji
- **Audio**: "10,000 passive subscribers or 1,000 true fans? Only one of these is a business."

## Body (Main Content)

### Scene 1 (5-15s)
- **Visual**: Animated comparison — left side: person scrolling past an email (subscriber). Right side: person forwarding email to three friends (fan)
- **Audio**: "A subscriber reads your content. A fan forwards it to three people and argues with strangers who criticize it. Most creators never build fans because they optimize for reach instead of depth."

### Scene 2 (15-25s)
- **Visual**: Four mistake cards flipping over one at a time with X marks
- **Audio**: "Four mistakes that keep you stuck: chasing follower counts, building on rented land, ignoring your first fifty supporters, and trying to be everywhere at once."

### Scene 3 (25-40s)
- **Visual**: Four strategy cards with icons: email icon, funnel diagram, handshake, gift box
- **Audio**: "Four strategies that work: Email as your foundation — you own the relationship. Social media as a funnel, not a home. Strategic collaborations that borrow trust. And rewarding the people who showed up first."

### Scene 4 (40-50s)
- **Visual**: TLDR stats appearing: "7M+ subscribers. 12 newsletters. 40%+ open rates" with text: "Built on: curate aggressively, deliver consistently, respect time"
- **Audio**: "The newsletter operators with the most loyal audiences share one quality: they'd rather lose a subscriber than waste one's time."

## Call to Action (50-60s)
- **Visual**: Text overlay: "Your first 100 fans recruit the next 1,000." with link
- **Audio**: "Stop measuring reach. Start measuring resonance. Full guide linked below."
