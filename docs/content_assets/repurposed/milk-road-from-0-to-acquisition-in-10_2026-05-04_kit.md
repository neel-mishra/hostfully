## Executive Summary

This case study analyzes Milk Road's rapid trajectory from launch to acquisition in the cryptocurrency newsletter space, covering February to December 2022. Starting with zero subscribers and no existing audience, the daily crypto newsletter reached 250,000+ subscribers and completed an acquisition within 10 months.

The growth strategy combined five channels: an automated referral program launched alongside the first issue, cross-promotional partnerships with complementary newsletters, paid acquisition on Facebook and TikTok optimized for 30-day retention rather than raw signup volume, content designed for daily 5-minute consumption with scannable formatting, and social media amplification directing traffic to the newsletter signup.

Key strategic decisions: no custom infrastructure (no-code platform with built-in growth tools), growth mechanics from day one rather than post-maturity, quality-per-acquisition measurement over cost-per-acquisition, and in-newsletter feedback loops that shaped editorial direction.

The principles — launch with growth mechanics in place, measure retention alongside acquisition, invest in format and reading experience, treat social media as an amplifier — apply across newsletter verticals beyond crypto. The differentiator was execution speed and willingness to ship before the product was polished.
