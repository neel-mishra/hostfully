# Milk Road: 0 to 250K Subscribers to Acquired in 10 Months <!-- pragma: allowlist secret -->

Milk Road started as a Twitter DM between Shaan Puri and Ben Levy. Ten months later, it had 250,000+ subscribers and got acquired.

That timeline is absurd. Most newsletters take years to reach that scale. Milk Road did it because the founders treated the newsletter as a product to sell from day one — not a passion project to figure out later.

## The Starting Point: Zero Everything

Shaan and Ben launched with no subscriber list, no content archive, and no brand. The crypto space in early 2022 was noisy — fragmented info, too much hype, not enough clarity. They bet that a short, fun, daily crypto newsletter could cut through.

The bet was right. But the content alone didn't get them to 250K.

## The Growth Playbook

Five things drove Milk Road's growth. Most newsletters do one or two. Milk Road ran all five simultaneously.

### Referrals that actually worked

Milk Road offered a free crypto investor PDF for a single referral. One referral. Low bar, clear reward. The automation handled tracking and delivery — no manual work. This turned readers into a distribution channel.

### Paid acquisition on Facebook and TikTok

They ran ads with memes and social proof, not polished brand creative. Then they watched the data closely — doubling down on channels with low cost-per-subscriber and cutting the rest fast.

### Newsletter cross-promotions

Milk Road partnered with other finance and tech newsletters to swap recommendations. This put them in front of audiences that were already newsletter readers — the highest-intent acquisition channel besides referrals.

### No-code infrastructure

No developers. They used a newsletter platform's built-in website builder, landing pages, and signup forms. This meant they could test and iterate without engineering bottlenecks.

### Reader polls for engagement

Embedded polls in every issue gave readers a reason to interact beyond just reading. The engagement data also told them what topics to cover next.

## Why It Worked

Milk Road wasn't doing anything novel. Referral programs, paid ads, cross-promos — these are known tactics. The difference was execution speed and focus.

Shaan Puri had an existing audience and understood internet distribution. They didn't build custom tools. They didn't hire a big team. They picked a platform, plugged in the growth levers, and spent every hour on content and acquisition.

The 250K number in under 10 months made them an obvious acquisition target. When you can show a repeatable subscriber growth engine and a highly engaged audience in a high-value niche, buyers notice.

## The Takeaway

Milk Road proves that newsletter growth isn't about having the best content. It's about having good content *and* a distribution system that compounds.

Most founders over-invest in writing and under-invest in referrals, paid acquisition, and partnerships. Milk Road did both — and built an asset worth acquiring in under a year.
