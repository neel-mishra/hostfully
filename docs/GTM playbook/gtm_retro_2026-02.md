## Executive Summary

February was a mixed month. Meta Ads drove 175 total leads at a blended CPA of $162, with the PMS Conversions Manual campaign emerging as the clear volume leader (94 leads, $79.88 CPA). The Guidebook Conversions campaign delivered the best efficiency among mid-volume campaigns at $85.49 CPA. Engagement campaigns (Boosted Posts, Post Promovido) drove strong brand visibility at very low CPCs ($0.51–$0.84) but limited direct conversions.

**Key wins:** PMS Manual prospecting scaled efficiently; Guidebook lead-gen delivered strong CPA; engagement content drove high reach at minimal cost.

**Key misses:** Google Ads API was unavailable (404 errors) — no cross-platform comparison possible. Ahrefs API key not configured — SEO performance untracked. Content pipeline execution lagged (15% completion rate). Several high-budget campaigns (Industry Report, PMS Retargeting) had expensive CPAs ($284–$711).

**Key shifts needed:** Fix Google Ads and Ahrefs API integrations for next cycle. Double down on PMS Manual and Guidebook campaigns. Accelerate content pipeline execution. Investigate high-CPA retargeting campaigns for optimization or pause.

## What Worked

1. **PMS Manual Prospecting (Testimonials ad set):** Delivered the best combination of volume and efficiency — 94 leads at $79.88 CPA. The Testimonials creative drove 87% of spend with a $3.79 CPC. Social proof-driven creative clearly resonates with the property management audience.

2. **Guidebook Conversions campaign:** 48 leads at $85.49 CPA. Strong mid-funnel performance converting interest into lead captures.

3. **Low-cost engagement (Boosted Posts + Post Promovido):** Combined $740 in spend drove 17,487 engagements and significant brand visibility. The Brazilian market (pt-br) shows strong engagement at $0.51 CPC.

4. **Content prioritization:** The team correctly executed on the highest-scored content items first (4.2 and 3.2 scores).

---

## What Didn't Work

1. **Industry Report Prospecting:** $4,977 for 7 leads ($711 CPA). The cost cap bid strategy is likely too restrictive, limiting optimization. The broad + LAL audience targeting may be too diffuse for a sales-objective campaign.

2. **CA-US PMS Prospecting (website-conv):** $3,997 for 7 leads ($571 CPA). Despite a $1,000/day budget, conversion volume is anemic. The website conversion objective may need better landing page optimization or the audience needs refinement.

3. **PMS Retargeting:** $3,975 for 14 leads at $284 CPA. Retargeting audiences should convert at lower CPAs. Likely audience fatigue or misaligned creative for warm audiences.

4. **Content pipeline velocity:** 15% completion rate is critically low. At this pace, the backlog will continue growing. Either the pipeline is too ambitious or execution capacity needs to increase.

5. **Google Ads + Ahrefs integrations:** Both APIs are non-functional, leaving major blind spots in cross-channel performance and organic measurement.

## Learnings & Implications for March

1. **Double down on Testimonials creative format.** The PMS Manual campaign's Testimonials ad set proved social proof drives the best performance. Expand this creative approach to other campaigns (Guidebook, Industry Report).

2. **Restructure or pause Industry Report campaign.** At $711 CPA, this campaign needs either a creative refresh, audience refinement, or should be paused in favor of reallocating budget to PMS Manual and Guidebook.

3. **Audit retargeting audiences.** Both PMS and AU-UK retargeting campaigns are underperforming. Check audience freshness, frequency caps, and creative rotation.

4. **Fix infrastructure gaps urgently.** Google Ads API and Ahrefs need to be operational for the next cycle. Without these, we're flying half-blind on paid performance and completely blind on organic.

5. **Accelerate content pipeline.** Prioritize the two 3.2-score articles ("Local Communities" and "Newsletter Swaps") and aim for at least 5 completions in March. Consider dedicating a sprint to clear the backlog.

6. **Explore Brazilian market expansion.** The pt-br engagement campaigns show strong interest at very low costs. Test a conversion-optimized campaign targeting the Brazilian property management market.

7. **Landing page optimization for website-conv campaigns.** The CA-US prospecting campaign has 90 LPVs from 423 link clicks (21% drop-off). Landing page load speed or relevance may be an issue.
