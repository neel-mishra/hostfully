## Executive Summary

February was a challenging month for paid acquisition. Meta Ads spend totaled $29,290 generating 166 leads at a blended CPL of $176.44 — a significant regression from January's $27,472 spend / 234 leads / $117.40 CPL. **Wins:** Meta lead campaigns (PMS_Conversions-Manual and Guidebook_Conversions) still drive the bulk of leads. Content pipeline delivered 3 high-quality blog posts. Site traffic held steady at ~7,830 sessions/week with 3.22% conversion rate.

**Misses:** Blended CPL up 50%+ MoM on Meta. Google Ads largely paused/inactive except for brand. CTR declined 26% WoW in latest week, suggesting creative fatigue. Ahrefs and Google Ads APIs unavailable — data gaps in this report.

**Key Shifts:** Several previously high-spend Meta campaigns (Industry Report Prospecting, PMS Retargeting) are now paused or underperforming. Brazil and Mexico geo expansions remain paused with zero activity.

## What Worked

1. **Meta-Leads-PMS_Conversions-Manual** remains the workhorse — 91 leads at $78.81 CPA, well below the account blended CPL. This campaign should be reactivated.
2. **Meta-Leads-Guidebook_Conversions** — 44 leads at $92.05 CPA demonstrates that lead magnet / guidebook offers convert efficiently.
3. **Brazil pt-br campaign** — Lowest CPC in the account ($1.47) with 3 leads at $175.76 CPA. LATAM geo shows promise at fraction of US/CA cost.
4. **Content pipeline delivered 3 blog posts** — All completed items are strategic/tactical pieces that align with the newsletter audience.
5. **Website conversion rate held at 3.22%** — Organic traffic is converting well despite not being fully optimized.
6. **Google Ads brand campaign** — 97.72% impression share, $201.95 CPL for brand terms. Quality Score 10 on core brand keywords.

## What Didn't Work

1. **ca-us_pms_prospecting_website-conv** — $5,145 spent for 7 leads ($735 CPA). This campaign is actively burning budget with poor lead conversion. Requires immediate creative or audience refresh.
2. **Meta-Sales-Industry Report_Prospecting** — $4,977 for 7 leads ($711 CPA). The industry report offer isn't resonating at top-of-funnel.
3. **au-uk_pms_prospecting_website-conv** — $1,701 spent with zero leads. AU/UK prospecting is not converting at current targeting/creative.
4. **CTR declining** — 26% WoW drop (0.96% vs 1.30%) suggests creative fatigue across the account.
5. **Content pipeline velocity** — Only 3/20 items completed (15%). Several high-scoring items remain untouched.
6. **Google Ads non-brand** — All non-brand campaigns paused, missing opportunity for non-brand lead generation.
7. **API availability** — Google Ads and Ahrefs APIs were both unavailable, limiting data-driven decision making.

## Learnings & Implications

1. **Lead-form campaigns outperform website-conversion campaigns on Meta.** PMS_Conversions-Manual (lead form) achieves $78.81 CPA vs. ca-us_pms_prospecting_website-conv at $735 CPA. March should shift budget back to lead forms.

2. **Guidebook/lead magnet offers are the second-best performing creative angle.** $92.05 CPA on Guidebook_Conversions. Consider scaling this with fresh creative.

3. **Retargeting ROI is mixed.** PMS retargeting ($342 CPA) underperformed expectations, but au-uk retargeting ($198.82 CPA for 2 leads) and ca-us retargeting ($875 CPA for 1 lead) are small-sample. Retargeting needs audience refresh with website visitors and engaged ad viewers.

4. **LATAM expansion shows early signal.** Brazil pt-br has the cheapest clicks ($1.47 CPC) and is generating meetings. Worth expanding cautiously.

5. **Creative refresh is overdue.** The 26% CTR decline is a clear signal. New ad formats, testimonial-based creatives, and video should be prioritized.

6. **Content pipeline needs acceleration.** 15% completion rate means the SEO compounding effect is being delayed. Prioritize the 4 items with weighted scores >= 3.2.

7. **Performance score (87%) needs addressing.** Below the 90% threshold — focus on LCP optimization to improve organic rankings.
