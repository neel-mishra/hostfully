# Prospect Brief: Wiz

## Company Overview
Wiz is a cloud security company offering a unified platform for cloud visibility, risk assessment, and threat detection across AWS, Azure, and GCP. Founded in 2020 by former Microsoft engineers, Wiz has grown to become the fastest-growing cybersecurity company in history, reaching $100M ARR in 18 months. Valued at $12B+ after their $1B Series E in May 2026, Wiz famously turned down a $23B acquisition offer from Google to pursue an independent IPO path. Their customers include 40%+ of the Fortune 100.

## Why TLDR
1. **Exact audience match.** Wiz sells to cloud security teams, DevOps engineers, and CISOs. TLDR InfoSec (340K subscribers, 44% open rate) and TLDR DevOps (250K subscribers, 43% open rate) reach precisely these personas every morning.
2. **Pre-IPO demand gen window.** Companies approaching IPO dramatically increase marketing spend to demonstrate growth metrics. TLDR offers a scalable, efficient channel to complement their LinkedIn and Google investments.
3. **CPC arbitrage.** Security keywords on LinkedIn run $12-20+ per click. TLDR delivers the same audience at roughly 50% lower CPCs, proven by case studies like Redact.
4. **Multi-newsletter, multi-persona campaigns.** Wiz can reach security practitioners (InfoSec), cloud engineers (DevOps), and tech executives (Tech) — covering their entire buying committee across TLDR's portfolio.

## Current Marketing Activity
- Heavy Google Ads investment on "cloud security platform," "CNAPP," "CSPM," and competitor comparison keywords
- Active LinkedIn Ads targeting CISOs, VP Security, and cloud engineering roles
- Content marketing through blog, webinars, and security research publications

## Newsletter Fit
- **TLDR InfoSec** (primary): 340K cybersecurity professionals at 44% open rate. Primary placement for reaching CISOs and security engineers.

- **Recommended package:** Multi-newsletter campaign spanning InfoSec + DevOps for targeted reach, with periodic Tech placements for executive awareness.
