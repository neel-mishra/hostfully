### Campaign Overview
This 3-step cold email sequence targets VPs of Marketing at B2B SaaS companies, leveraging the hypothesis that their rapid growth (indicated by a hiring spree or other expansion) is leading to "growth chaos" and ops debt in their marketing channels. The goal is to agitate around the pain points of scaling marketing effectively (especially paid social) and position TLDR as a high-ROI, high-attention alternative for reaching tech professionals. Each email is concise, plain-text, and conversational, focusing on TLDR's advertiser-facing pillars, primarily **Pillar 4: Outperform Paid Social (ROI)**.

### Clay AI Enrichment Prompt (For First Line Generation)
```prompt
Analyze [Company Name]'s recent public information (press releases, news articles from the last 12 months, and LinkedIn company page) for indicators of significant growth or strategic focus. Prioritize finding:
1.  Announcements of recent funding rounds (e.g., Series A, B, C, D).
2.  Mentions of significant hiring initiatives or team expansions, particularly within marketing, engineering, or product departments.
3.  Major product launches, significant feature updates, or new market entries.
4.  Strategic partnerships or significant company milestones.

Based on this analysis, generate a single, concise opening sentence (maximum 25 words) for a cold email. This sentence should:
a.  Acknowledge a *specific* recent growth event or strategic focus.
b.  Maintain a professional and observant tone.
c.  Seamlessly lead into a discussion about the challenges of scaling marketing efficiently.

If specific recent growth events are not clearly identifiable, default to a general observation about their industry or stated company goals (e.g., "saw [Company Name] is focused on scaling efficiently this year").

Examples of desired output formats:
-   If funding: "Congrats on your recent Series B, [Company Name]!"
-   If hiring: "Noticed the recent growth of your marketing team at [Company Name]."
-   If product launch: "Impressed by the recent [Product Name] launch from [Company Name]."
-   Default: "Saw [Company Name] is focused on scaling efficiently this year."
```
