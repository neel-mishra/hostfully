# Business Context: TLDR

## 1. Executive Intent
*   **Mission Statement:** To keep tech professionals informed in the most time-efficient way possible — a free, curated daily briefing that respects the reader's time and delivers only what matters.
*   **Vision Statement:** The default morning read for every tech professional on the planet — and the highest-ROI advertising channel for companies that want to reach them.
*   **The "Aha" Moment:** When a subscriber realizes they've been fully caught up on AI, startups, dev tools, and big tech news in 5 minutes — before their first meeting of the day.

## 2. Business Model & Fundamentals
*   **Revenue Model:** 100% advertising-supported. Newsletters are free forever for readers. Revenue comes from sponsored native ads placed in newsletters.
*   **The 2-Sided Network:**

    | Side | Who | Value Received | Value Created |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Readers (Demand Side)** | 7M+ tech professionals (developers, PMs, engineers, executives, marketers, founders) | Free, curated tech news in 5 minutes/day | Attention, engagement, high open rates (40-48%), and purchase intent for advertisers |
    | **Advertisers (Supply Side)** | B2B tech companies (SaaS, cloud, dev tools, AI, cybersecurity) | High-intent audience, 50% lower CPC than LinkedIn, up to 52x ROI | Revenue that funds content curation, editorial quality, and subscriber growth |

    **Network Effect:** More readers → higher open rates → more advertiser demand → more revenue → better content & growth investment → more readers. The flywheel accelerates as the audience becomes more valuable to advertisers, which funds the content that attracts more readers.

*   **Ad Placement Tiers:**
    *   **Primary Placement:** Top of newsletter. Logo, up to 100 words, 3 CTAs. Premium pricing (up to $30K/day for flagship).
    *   **Secondary Placement:** Middle of newsletter. Up to 50 words, 2 CTAs.
    *   **Quick Links:** Bottom of newsletter. Up to 30 words, 1 CTA.
*   **Included Services:** Copywriting (TLDR's team writes ad copy to match editorial voice) and campaign performance reporting at no additional cost.
*   **North Star Metrics:**
    *   **Reader side:** Total subscribers, daily open rate, time-to-read satisfaction
    *   **Advertiser side:** Revenue per newsletter, advertiser retention rate, attributed ROI for sponsors

## 4. Strategic Roadmap
*   **Phase 1 (Subscriber Growth):** Grow total subscriber base through paid acquisition (LinkedIn, Meta, Google), referral programs, and SEO. Every new subscriber increases ad inventory value.
*   **Phase 2 (Advertiser Expansion):** Expand beyond tech-native advertisers into adjacent verticals (fintech, education, recruiting, enterprise software). Increase advertiser retention and upsell across newsletter portfolio.
*   **Phase 3 (Platform Evolution):** Deepen audience data, launch self-serve ad buying, introduce performance-based pricing tiers, and explore premium content or community products.

---
### AI Agent Context Rule:
TLDR operates a **2-sided marketplace**. Every piece of content must consider WHICH side of the network it's targeting:
-   **Reader-facing content:** Emphasize **time savings**, **curation quality**, **free forever**, and **professional credibility** ("what 1.6M tech professionals read").
-   **Advertiser-facing content:** Emphasize **ROI**, **audience quality**, **lower CPC than LinkedIn/Google/Meta**, **open rates**, and **dedicated support**.
-   Never confuse the two audiences. A subscriber acquisition ad should never mention advertising pricing. An advertiser pitch should never read like a newsletter signup page.
