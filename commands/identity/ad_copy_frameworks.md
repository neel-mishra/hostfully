# Ad Copy Frameworks: TLDR

TLDR runs paid ads for two distinct purposes. The copy frameworks, angles, and compliance rules differ completely between them.

---

## Part 1: Reader Acquisition (Subscriber Growth)

Goal: Drive newsletter signups from tech professionals. The product is free — the barrier is awareness and trust, not price.

### 1.1 Google Ads (Search / Intent)
*   **The Strategy:** Capture people actively searching for tech news, newsletters, or specific content topics.
*   **Optimization Goal:** High CTR + low cost-per-subscriber via benefit-first headlines.

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headlines** | "Free Tech Newsletter", "5-Min Daily Tech Briefing", "Read by 1.6M Professionals" | Lead with "Free" + time value + social proof. |
| **Descriptions** | "Get the most important tech news in a free 5-minute daily email. AI, startups, dev tools & more. Join 1.6M readers." | Combine curation promise + specific topics + subscriber count. |

#### Agent Execution Logic: Google (Reader)
*   **Keyword clusters:** "tech newsletter," "daily tech news email," "AI newsletter," "developer newsletter," "startup news," "free tech briefing"
*   **Competitor conquesting:** Bid on "Morning Brew tech," "Hacker News newsletter," "The Hustle alternative"
*   **Data-First Headlines:** Use numbers. "1,600,000+ Subscribers." "5 Minutes. Every Morning."

---

### 1.2 Meta Ads (Disruption / Awareness)
*   **The Strategy:** Interrupt the scroll with a compelling visual + hook that makes the reader think "I need this in my life." Visual-first, benefit-driven.
*   **Tone:** Clean, confident, slightly clever. Not corporate.

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Text** | The "time saved" comparison or peer pressure angle. | "Your CTO reads this before their first meeting. Tech news in 5 minutes — free." |
| **Headline** | The big stat or bold claim. | "1,600,000+ Tech Pros Start Here" |
| **Creative** | Clean static with bold stat, or split-screen (chaos vs. TLDR). | Show the value visually: "5 min" in oversized type, subscriber count, inbox mockup. |

#### Agent Execution Logic: Meta (Reader)
*   **Cold prospecting:** Lead with time-saving hook + social proof. Interest targeting: technology, programming, startups, SaaS.
*   **Retargeting:** "Still spending 30 minutes on Hacker News? There's a faster way."
*   **Lookalike:** Build from existing subscriber list. 1% for quality, 3-5% for scale.

---

### 1.3 LinkedIn Ads (B2B / Professional)
*   **The Strategy:** Professional credibility. Appeal to the desire to be informed, stay sharp, and keep up with peers.
*   **Archetype:** The "peer-endorsed briefing."

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro Text** | Peer pressure + utility. | "1,600,000 tech professionals start their day with TLDR. AI, startups, dev tools — curated in 5 minutes." |
| **Headline** | Authority or time savings. | "What 1.6M Tech Leaders Read Every Morning" |

#### Agent Execution Logic: LinkedIn (Reader)
*   **Persona matching:**
    *   **To Engineers:** Talk about "staying current" and "developer news."
    *   **To Executives:** Talk about "strategic awareness" and "what your team is reading."
*   **Social proof:** Always include subscriber count and "free" in the first line.

---

### 1.4 Reddit / X / Community Channels (Reader)
*   **The Strategy:** Native, conversational, community-aware. These platforms punish anything that feels like advertising.
*   **Tone:** Casual, authentic, slightly self-deprecating. "We made a newsletter. People seem to like it."

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reddit** | Value proposition without hype. | "Free daily email. Tech news in 5 min. No spam. 1.6M subscribers." |
| **X / Twitter** | Conversational, topical. | Hook into trending tech topics + "we covered this in TLDR today" with signup link. |

## Part 2: Advertiser Acquisition (Revenue Growth)

Goal: Convince B2B tech marketers to buy ad placements in TLDR newsletters. The barrier is proving ROI vs. existing channels (LinkedIn, Google, Meta).

### 2.1 Google Ads (Search / Intent)
*   **The Strategy:** Capture marketers searching for newsletter advertising, B2B lead gen channels, or alternatives to expensive platforms.

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Headlines** | "Advertise to 7M Tech Pros", "Newsletter Ads That Work", "Lower CPC Than LinkedIn" | Lead with audience size + performance comparison. |
| **Descriptions** | "Reach developers, PMs, and CTOs with native ads in TLDR newsletters. 50% lower CPC than LinkedIn. Copywriting included." | Combine audience + CPC advantage + included services. |

#### Agent Execution Logic: Google (Advertiser)
*   **Keyword clusters:** "newsletter advertising," "reach developers," "B2B tech advertising," "developer marketing channels," "LinkedIn ad alternatives"
*   **Data-First:** "52x ROI." "$1M Pipeline." "50% Lower CPC."

---

### 2.2 LinkedIn Ads (B2B / Decision-Maker)
*   **The Strategy:** Reach the growth marketers and CMOs who control ad budgets. Ironic channel choice — use LinkedIn to sell them on moving budget FROM LinkedIn to TLDR.
*   **Archetype:** The case study proof point.

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Intro Text** | Lead with a case study stat. | "Delve drove $1M in attributed pipeline — 52x ROI — through TLDR ads. Your LinkedIn campaigns doing that?" |
| **Headline** | The comparison hook. | "50% Lower CPC Than LinkedIn. See the Data." |

#### Agent Execution Logic: LinkedIn (Advertiser)
*   **Persona matching:**
    *   **To Growth Marketers:** Talk about CPL, pipeline, attribution.
    *   **To CMOs:** Talk about channel diversification, ROI, and board-level metrics.
*   **Always include:** Case study reference + "See the case studies" CTA.

---

### 2.3 Direct Outbound / Email (Advertiser)
*   **The Strategy:** Targeted outreach to marketing leaders at companies that match the advertiser ICP. Reference their specific audience overlap with TLDR.

#### Copy Assets
| Asset Type | Focus | Best Practice |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subject Line** | Specific + data. | "How [Similar Company] drove $382K pipeline through TLDR" |
| **Body** | 3 sentences max. | Audience overlap + relevant case study + CTA to book a call. |

## 3. Compliance & Quality (Both Sides)

### Reader Acquisition
*   **No misleading claims:** Don't promise career advancement, salary increases, or "insider secrets." We promise curated tech news, fast.
*   **Always say "Free":** The product is free. This should be prominent in every ad. Don't make readers wonder about pricing.
*   **No spam positioning:** Never imply frequency beyond what's true (1 email/day per newsletter). Don't say "we'll never email you" — we email daily, that's the product.

### Advertiser Acquisition
*   **Case studies must be accurate:** Only use verified, approved case study numbers. No rounding up. No extrapolating.
*   **CPC comparisons must be fair:** When saying "50% lower CPC than LinkedIn," this comes from specific advertiser reports — don't generalize to all advertisers.
*   **No guaranteed results:** We can share case studies and benchmarks, but never promise specific ROI, CPL, or pipeline numbers for a prospective advertiser.

---

### AI Agent Implementation Rule:
When drafting ad copy, always produce variations across these angles:

**Reader acquisition (3 angles):**
1.  **The "Time Saved" Angle:** Focus on 5 minutes, efficiency, no scrolling.
2.  **The "Peer Proof" Angle:** Focus on 1.6M subscribers, "your CTO reads this," belonging.
3.  **The "Curation" Angle:** Focus on editorial quality, no fluff, no algorithm, human-curated.

**Advertiser acquisition (3 angles):**
1.  **The "ROI" Angle:** Focus on case study numbers — pipeline, ROI multiplier, CPC.
2.  **The "Audience" Angle:** Focus on 7M tech professionals, role breakdown, open rates.
3.  **The "Channel" Angle:** Focus on comparison vs. LinkedIn/Google/Meta — lower cost, higher quality, less noise.
