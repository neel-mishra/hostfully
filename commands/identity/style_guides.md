# Style Guides: TLDR

## 1. Editorial Style (The "Curator" Voice)
*   **Audience:** Newsletter readers — developers, engineers, PMs, executives, founders.
*   **Goal:** Trust and consistency. Readers should recognize TLDR's voice immediately — informational, concise, slightly wry.
*   **Key Phrases:** "In a nutshell," "TLDR," "X minute read," "The big picture."

### Editorial Best Practices
| Element | Standard | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Brevity** | Every word earns its place. Cut the setup, lead with the news. | Not: "In an interesting development today..." → Yes: "GitHub was compromised via AI triage bot." |
| **Neutrality** | Report, don't editorialize. Let the reader form their own take. | Not: "This is terrible for privacy." → Yes: "The implications for user privacy are being debated." |
| **Accessibility** | Explain jargon when the audience might not know it. Use parenthetical definitions. | "LLMs (Large Language Models) are being used to..." |

---

## 2. Growth Marketing Style (The "Subscriber Magnet" Voice)
*   **Audience:** Prospective subscribers seeing ads on LinkedIn, Meta, Google, Reddit, X.
*   **Goal:** Stop the scroll. Communicate value in under 3 seconds. Drive signups.
*   **Key Phrases:** "5 minutes," "Free," "1,600,000+ readers," "Keep up with tech," "No spam."

### Growth Best Practices
| Element | Standard | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lead with the benefit** | Don't describe the product. Describe what the reader gets. | Not: "TLDR is a curated newsletter." → Yes: "Tech news in 5 minutes. Free." |
| **Social proof first** | Large subscriber counts build instant trust. | "Join 1,600,000 tech professionals." |
| **Remove friction** | Emphasize "free" and "one email per day." Address the unspoken objection (spam). | "One email. Every morning. Unsubscribe anytime." |

---

## 3. Advertiser Sales Style (The "Performance Partner" Voice)
*   **Audience:** Growth marketers, demand gen managers, CMOs at B2B tech companies.
*   **Goal:** Prove ROI. Differentiate from LinkedIn/Google/Meta. Close the deal.
*   **Key Phrases:** "52x ROI," "50% lower CPC," "$1M in pipeline," "7M+ tech professionals," "40-48% open rate."

### Advertiser Best Practices
| Element | Standard | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lead with data** | Every claim needs a number. Case studies are currency. | Not: "TLDR works great." → Yes: "Delve drove $1M in pipeline. 52x ROI." |
| **Compare to known benchmarks** | Marketers think in CPCs and CPLs. Give them apples-to-apples. | "50% lower CPC than your LinkedIn campaigns." |
| **Remove risk** | Emphasize included services and support. | "Copywriting and performance reports included. Dedicated account manager." |

---

## 4. Style Comparison Matrix
*Translation layer between editorial, growth, and advertiser contexts.*

| Concept | Editorial (Newsletter) | Growth (Subscriber Ads) | Advertiser (Sales) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Who reads TLDR** | "Our readers" | "1,600,000+ tech professionals" | "7M+ developers, PMs, and executives" |
| **Why it works** | "Curated by editors" | "5 minutes. No fluff." | "40-48% open rate. 3 ads max per issue." |
| **The value** | "Stay informed" | "Keep up with tech" | "Reach your exact ICP at half the CPC" |
| **The tone** | Informational, neutral | Confident, clean, benefit-driven | Professional, data-forward, ROI-focused |

---

## 5. Internal Writing Style (PRDs & Comms)
*   **Philosophy:** "Scan in 2 minutes, act on immediately."
*   **Standards:**
    *   **No fluff:** If it doesn't inform a decision, cut it.
    *   **Metrics first:** Open every doc with the relevant numbers.
    *   **Link, don't copy:** Reference dashboards and source docs. Don't paste tables that go stale.

---

### AI Agent Implementation Rule:
*   **Newsletter content:** Use Editorial style (Section 1). Neutral, concise, informational.
*   **Subscriber acquisition ads:** Use Growth style (Section 2). Benefit-first, social proof, "free."
*   **Advertiser sales content:** Use Advertiser style (Section 3). Data-first, ROI, case studies.
*   **Internal docs:** Brief, honest, metrics-led (Section 5).
*   **Never mix styles.** A newsletter article should never read like a growth ad. An advertiser pitch should never read like editorial.
