# Internal Communication Style: TLDR Team

## 1. The Core Philosophy
*   **Standard:** Async-first, concise, transparent. We build a product that saves people time — our internal communication should reflect that.
*   **Philosophy:** "Respect the reader's time." If you can say it in one sentence, don't write three. If it's already documented, link to it.
*   **Execution:** Clear writing = clear thinking. Every internal doc should be scannable in under 2 minutes.

---

## 2. Tone & Diction
*   **Personality:** **Curious Editor.** We're curators at heart — always scanning for signal, cutting noise, and surfacing what matters. Internally, that means direct communication with context.
*   **Technicality:** Medium-high. Everyone at TLDR should understand newsletter metrics (open rate, CTR, CPM), basic ad operations, and growth mechanics.
*   **Transparency:** Open by default. Revenue numbers, subscriber metrics, advertiser feedback, and churn data should flow freely. Bad news shared early is fixable. Bad news hidden is a crisis.

---

## 3. The "This, Not That" Internal Framework
| We Are... | But Never... | Why? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Editorial-Minded** | Clickbaity | We curate with integrity. Internal comms should be just as honest. |
| **Data-Informed** | Paralyzed by Data | Metrics guide decisions, they don't make them. Ship, measure, iterate. |
| **Lean & Fast** | Sloppy | 22 people serving 7M readers means every person matters. Move quickly but don't cut corners on quality. |
| **Reader-First** | Advertiser-Captive | Revenue comes from advertisers, but the product is for readers. Never compromise editorial quality for a sponsor. |

---

## 4. Structured Messaging Templates

### The "Metric Alert" (Subscriber/Engagement)
*   **What changed:** "TLDR AI open rate dropped 3 points this week (45% → 42%)."
*   **Hypothesis:** "Subject line testing — the shorter variants underperformed."
*   **Next step:** "Reverting to long-form subject lines for the next 5 issues and re-evaluating."

### The "Advertiser Feedback" (Revenue Side)
*   **Client:** "[Company Name] — Primary Placement in TLDR Tech."
*   **Result:** "Generated 450 clicks at $3.80 CPC. Client expected $5+ CPC from LinkedIn benchmark."
*   **Follow-up:** "Sending performance report + proposing 3-issue renewal across TLDR Tech + TLDR AI."

### The "Growth Update" (Acquisition)
*   **Channel:** "LinkedIn subscriber acquisition campaign."
*   **Numbers:** "1,200 new subscribers this week at $1.85 CPA. Open rate of new cohort: 52% (above baseline)."
*   **Insight:** "The 'CTO already reads' hook outperformed 'free newsletter' hook by 40%."

---

## 5. Communication Rituals
*   **Standups:** Async. Post blockers and wins. Keep it to 3 bullet points max.
*   **Weekly Review:** Key metrics dashboard — subscribers, open rates, revenue, advertiser pipeline. No slides. Just the numbers and what they mean.
*   **Content Review:** Daily editorial decisions are made by editors with autonomy. Escalate only for brand/legal concerns.

---

### AI Agent Context Rule:
Internal communications should be **brief, data-backed, and actionable**. Assume competence. Don't over-explain metrics the team already knows. Link to source data instead of copying entire tables.
