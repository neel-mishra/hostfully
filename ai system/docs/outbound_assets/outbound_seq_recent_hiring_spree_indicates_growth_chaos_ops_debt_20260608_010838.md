### Campaign Overview
This 3-step cold email sequence targets VPs of Marketing at B2B SaaS companies, leveraging the hypothesis that recent rapid hiring sprees often lead to operational chaos or "ops debt." The sequence aims to agitate this potential pain point, offer a solution through social proof, and conclude with a low-friction breakup email. The strategy prioritizes personalization through a Clay AI-generated opening line for Email 1, ensuring a hyper-relevant hook.

### Clay AI Enrichment Prompt (For First Line Generation)
```prompt
"Based on the prospect's company name and available public data (LinkedIn company page, news, press releases, funding announcements within the last 12 months), generate a single, hyper-personalized opening line for a cold email.

The objective is to acknowledge recent company growth (specifically a hiring spree or significant team expansion) and subtly hint at the operational complexity or 'debt' this growth might create for a VP of Marketing.

**Instructions for AI:**
1.  **Scan for Growth Signals:** Identify any explicit mentions of recent hiring, team expansion, substantial headcount increase, new departmental growth (especially marketing, sales, or operations), or recent funding that would fuel such growth.
2.  **Extract Specific Evidence:** Pinpoint the exact phrasing or numbers if available (e.g., "expanded team by 15," "welcomed 10 new engineers and marketers," "post-Series B hiring").
3.  **Formulate Opening Line:**
    *   Start by directly referencing the observed growth.
    *   Immediately connect this growth to a potential challenge for a VP of Marketing regarding operational alignment or managing new processes.
    *   The line must be conversational, under 20 words, and designed to seamlessly transition into a discussion about 'simplifying growth operations.'

**Example Output:**
*   "noticed [company name]'s impressive hiring spree - that kind of growth often means juggling a lot of new marketing systems."
*   "congrats on expanding your marketing team so rapidly! keeping all those new moving parts aligned can be a big lift."
*   "saw the news about your recent funding and growth – brilliant! that scale often brings new operational complexities for VPs."
```
