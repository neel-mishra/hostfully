# HubSpot Marketing Hub — Comparison

## Overview
HubSpot Marketing Hub is a comprehensive marketing automation platform designed to help businesses attract, engage, and delight customers. As a core component of HubSpot's CRM Platform, it offers a wide range of tools including email marketing, landing page creation, SEO recommendations, social media management, blog publishing, ad management, and marketing automation workflows. It is particularly known for its user-friendly interface and seamless integration with HubSpot CRM, making it an attractive option for businesses seeking an all-in-one solution to manage their sales and marketing efforts from a single platform. HubSpot Marketing Hub is tiered into Free, Starter, Professional, and Enterprise plans, with feature sets and pricing scaling significantly across these levels to accommodate businesses of various sizes and complexities.

## Migration Guide
Switching from HubSpot Marketing Hub to our platform is a streamlined process designed to minimize downtime and ensure a smooth transition. Follow these steps:

1.  **Data Export from HubSpot:**
    *   **Contacts:** Export all contact lists, including associated properties, from your HubSpot CRM.
    *   **Companies & Deals:** If utilizing HubSpot CRM, export company and deal data to maintain associated records.
    *   **Email Templates & Campaigns:** While direct migration of complex email layouts is challenging, export HTML/CSS of your most critical templates and gather performance data.
    *   **Landing Pages & Website Pages:** Export HTML or content of key pages. Consider using a crawler for content extraction.
    *   **Workflows:** Document your existing HubSpot workflows meticulously, including triggers, actions, and delays. This is crucial for re-building on our platform.
    *   **Files & Images:** Download all marketing assets (images, PDFs, videos) from your HubSpot file manager.
2.  **Platform Setup & Configuration:**
    *   **Account Provisioning:** Our onboarding team will set up your new account and guide you through initial configurations.
    *   **Domain & DNS Setup:** Configure your sending domains, tracking domains, and necessary DNS records (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) with our support team to ensure optimal email deliverability and tracking.
3.  **Data Import to Our Platform:**
    *   **Contacts & Companies:** Utilize our robust import tools to upload your exported CSV files. Map HubSpot properties to our platform's fields, or create custom fields as needed.
    *   **Marketing Assets:** Upload your images, files, and re-create landing pages and email templates using our intuitive builders.
4.  **Re-building Workflows & Automations:**
    *   Based on your documented HubSpot workflows, re-build your marketing automation sequences within our platform. Leverage our advanced logic and customization features to potentially enhance their effectiveness.
5.  **Integration Configuration:**
    *   Connect any third-party tools (e.g., CRM, advertising platforms, analytics) using our native integrations or API capabilities.
6.  **Testing & Go-Live:**
    *   Thoroughly test all campaigns, forms, landing pages, and automations to ensure everything functions as expected.
    *   Once confident, redirect traffic from old HubSpot assets to their new counterparts on our platform.

Our dedicated migration specialists are available to provide hands-on assistance throughout this process, ensuring a seamless and efficient transition.
