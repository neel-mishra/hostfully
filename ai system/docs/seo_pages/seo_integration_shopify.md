## Migration Guide
Integrating your Shopify store with our marketing automation platform is a straightforward process designed to get you up and running quickly:

### 1. Connect Your Shopify Store
*   Navigate to the "Integrations" section within our platform.
*   Select "Shopify" and follow the prompts to authorize the connection. This typically involves installing our app from the Shopify App Store or entering your Shopify store URL and granting necessary API permissions.

### 2. Import Your Data
*   Once connected, our platform will automatically begin syncing your historical customer data, order information, product catalog, and abandoned cart data from Shopify.
*   Allow time for the initial sync to complete, which varies depending on the volume of your data.

### 3. Map Customer Fields & Segments
*   Review how your Shopify customer attributes (e.g., tags, custom fields) are mapped to fields within our platform. Adjust mappings as needed to ensure data consistency.
*   Recreate your existing Shopify customer segments (or create new, more advanced ones) using our platform's robust segmentation tools, leveraging the newly synced data.

### 4. Transfer & Create Marketing Assets
*   Migrate any existing email templates or content from your previous marketing tools or directly from Shopify's notification system.
*   Begin building your automated campaigns, flows, and individual marketing messages (email, SMS, etc.) within our platform, utilizing the Shopify data for personalization.

### 5. Go Live & Optimize
*   Test your integrations and campaigns thoroughly.
*   Deactivate any redundant marketing automations or notifications within Shopify (or other previous apps) to avoid duplicate communications.
*   Activate your new campaigns and monitor performance using our advanced analytics dashboard. Leverage A/B testing features to continuously optimize for better results.
