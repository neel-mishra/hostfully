## Migration Guide
Switching your advanced marketing and CRM operations from disparate tools or basic Shopify functionalities to our integrated platform is a streamlined process designed for minimal disruption.

1.  **Data Export from Shopify**:
    *   **Customer Data**: Export your customer list, including names, emails, addresses, and any custom attributes, directly from your Shopify admin panel (`Customers > Export`).
    *   **Order History**: Download your complete order history from `Orders > Export`.
    *   **Product Catalog**: Export your product data, including SKUs, descriptions, prices, and images, from `Products > Export`.
    *   *Tip*: Ensure all CSV files are clean and correctly formatted for easy import.

2.  **Import to Our Platform**:
    *   Utilize our platform's built-in data import tools. Upload your exported Shopify customer, order, and product data. Our system will guide you through mapping fields to ensure accuracy.
    *   *Estimated Time*: 1-3 hours, depending on data volume.

3.  **Connect Shopify Integration**:
    *   Navigate to the 'Integrations' section within our platform.
    *   Select 'Shopify' and follow the prompts to authorize the connection. This typically involves entering your Shopify store URL and approving permissions via the Shopify admin.
    *   This establishes a real-time, two-way sync for new customer data, orders, and product updates.
    *   *Estimated Time*: 15-30 minutes.

4.  **Configure and Test Workflows**:
    *   Recreate your essential marketing campaigns and customer journeys using our advanced automation builder.
    *   Test key touchpoints (e.g., welcome series, abandoned cart reminders) to ensure data flows correctly and messages are delivered as expected.
    *   *Estimated Time*: 2-5 days, depending on complexity and number of active campaigns.

5.  **Go Live**:
    *   Once testing is complete and you're confident in the setup, activate your campaigns and automation workflows. Our dedicated support team is available to assist throughout the migration process.
