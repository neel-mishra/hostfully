## Migration Guide
Integrating our platform with your existing Shopify store is designed to be seamless, enhancing your marketing capabilities without disrupting your core e-commerce operations.

1.  **Connect Your Shopify Store:** Navigate to our platform's integration settings and select "Shopify." You will be prompted to authorize the connection via your Shopify admin panel. This grants secure API access for data synchronization.
2.  **Initial Data Synchronization:** Our platform will automatically initiate a historical sync of your customer data, order history, product catalog, and relevant events from Shopify. This typically takes a few minutes to a few hours depending on the volume of your data.
3.  **Map Custom Data Fields (Optional):** If you collect unique customer data points within Shopify or other systems, use our intuitive mapping interface to ensure these fields are correctly synchronized and contribute to unified customer profiles.
4.  **Configure Initial Campaigns & Automations:** Leverage our pre-built templates or design custom marketing automations that utilize your newly unified Shopify data. Start with core campaigns like welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, or post-purchase follow-ups.
5.  **Test and Launch:** Thoroughly test your integrations and campaigns to ensure data flows correctly and messages are delivered as intended. Once verified, launch your enhanced marketing operations, continuously monitoring performance and optimizing based on the new insights available.

## FAQ
### How does your platform integrate with my existing Shopify store?
Our platform connects directly to your Shopify store via a secure, official API integration. This allows for real-time synchronization of customer data, orders, products, and events, ensuring your marketing efforts are always based on the most current information.

### Can I replace Shopify with your platform for my e-commerce operations?
No, our platform is designed to *enhance* your Shopify store's marketing capabilities, not replace its core e-commerce functions. Shopify remains your powerful e-commerce engine for storefront, product management, and checkout, while our platform provides the advanced marketing automation, personalization, and analytics layer.

### What kind of customer data does your platform pull from Shopify?
We sync essential customer data (e.g., names, contact info, purchase history, order values), product details, inventory status, and event data (e.g., abandoned carts, product views) to build comprehensive customer profiles and power your marketing strategies.

### Will integrating your platform slow down my Shopify store's performance?
No, our integration is designed to be lightweight and asynchronous, meaning it operates in the background without impacting your Shopify store's frontend performance or customer experience.

### Is my Shopify data secure when integrated with your platform?
Yes, data security is paramount. We adhere to industry-leading security protocols and compliance standards (e.g., GDPR, CCPA) to ensure your Shopify customer data is always protected during transfer, storage, and processing.
