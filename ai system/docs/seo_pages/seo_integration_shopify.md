# Shopify — Integration

## Overview
Shopify is a leading global e-commerce platform, empowering businesses of all sizes to build, manage, and scale online stores. It provides a comprehensive suite of tools for product management, inventory, payments, order processing, and a foundational level of marketing capabilities. For marketing professionals, Shopify offers robust data on customer purchases, browsing behavior, and store performance, making it an essential data source for understanding customer journeys. Our platform integrates directly with Shopify to ingest this rich transactional and behavioral data, allowing brands to move beyond Shopify's native marketing functionalities and achieve advanced customer segmentation, multi-channel campaign orchestration, and AI-driven personalization at scale. This integration transforms raw Shopify data into actionable customer intelligence, enabling sophisticated lifecycle marketing strategies that drive higher customer lifetime value (LTV) and conversion rates.

## Migration Guide
Enhancing your Shopify store with our platform is a seamless process designed for rapid deployment and immediate impact.
1.  **Connect Your Shopify Store:** Begin by authenticating your Shopify store within our platform's integration settings. This typically involves a few clicks to grant necessary API permissions.
2.  **Initial Data Sync:** Our platform will automatically initiate a comprehensive sync of your historical customer data, order history, product catalog, and relevant behavioral data from Shopify. This ensures your existing customer base and product information are immediately available.
3.  **Map Custom Fields & Attributes:** Review and map any custom customer attributes or order details unique to your Shopify setup to corresponding fields within our platform for enhanced segmentation and personalization.
4.  **Install Tracking Script (Optional but Recommended):** Deploy our lightweight JavaScript tracking script on your Shopify storefront. This allows for real-time capture of granular website behavior (page views, product views, add-to-cart events) not always available via API.
5.  **Configure Initial Journeys:** Utilize our pre-built templates for common e-commerce automations (e.g., welcome series, abandoned cart recovery, post-purchase follow-ups) and customize them using your newly synced Shopify data.
6.  **Launch & Optimize:** Begin activating your first campaigns and leverage our robust analytics to monitor performance, A/B test elements, and continuously optimize your customer engagement strategies.
