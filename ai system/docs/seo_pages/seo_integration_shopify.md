1.  **Connect Your Shopify Store:** Navigate to the "Integrations" section within our platform. Select "Shopify" and follow the secure OAuth authentication process to grant read/write access to your store's customer, order, and product data. This typically takes less than 5 minutes.
2.  **Initial Data Sync & Profile Unification:** Our system will initiate a full sync of your historical customer and order data from Shopify. This data is then automatically processed and unified with any existing customer profiles from other sources (e.g., POS, website forms) to create comprehensive 360-degree views.

    *   **Automations:** Rebuild your basic Shopify automations (e.g., abandoned cart) using our powerful workflow canvas, adding new conditional logic and multi-channel steps.
5.  **Launch & Optimize:** Begin launching new campaigns and automations. Monitor performance within our analytics dashboard, leveraging AI-powered insights to continuously optimize your marketing strategy. Our dedicated support team is available to assist with any questions during this transition.
