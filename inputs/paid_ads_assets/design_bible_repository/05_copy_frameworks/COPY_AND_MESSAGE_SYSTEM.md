# Copy and Message Frameworks

## Messaging Spine
Most effective Hostfully paid-ad copy uses one of four message jobs:
1. **Outcome amplification** (`double`, `2x`, `scaled 350x`)
2. **Pain interruption** (`stuck in spreadsheets?`)
3. **Proof validation** (`700+ properties`, case stories)
4. **Revenue expansion** (`earn passive STR income`, referral lift)

## Funnel-Aligned Frameworks

## Top-of-Funnel (Awareness)
**Pattern:** `What if [pain context] could [large upside]?`
- Example structure:
  - `What if you could double your [business outcome]?`
  - `What if your [asset/workflow] could earn you [specific gain]?`

**Notes**
- Use curiosity and possibility.
- Avoid technical jargon.
- Emphasize one clear upside token.

## Mid-Funnel (Consideration)
**Pattern:** `See how [you/team] can [proof-backed uplift]`
- Example structure:
  - `See how you can 2x your [target metric]`
  - `How [peer brand/persona] scaled [X] without [costly tradeoff]`

**Notes**
- Introduce mechanism or case context.
- Keep outcome quantifiable.

## Bottom-Funnel (Conversion)
**Pattern:** `Pain acknowledgement + immediate help CTA`
- Example:
  - `Is your [business type] stuck in spreadsheets?`
  - CTA: `Let us help`

**Notes**
- Maximize relevance and urgency.
- Keep CTA short and supportive.

## Headline Construction Rules
- Ideal line count: `3-6` lines depending on aspect ratio.
- Include one high-salience anchor:
  - numerical (`2x`, `350x`, `8%`, `700+`)
  - emotional (`support system you deserve`)
  - pain (`stuck in spreadsheets`)
- Use bold selectively on one or two tokens only.

## Terminology Mapping
- Regional localization:
  - `vacation rental` (US-centric)
  - `holiday letting` (UK/EU-centric)
- Keep structure constant while swapping geo-language.

## CTA Taxonomy
- **Assistive:** `Let us help`
- **Proof/story:** `Read the Story`, `Read Yossi's Story`
- **Actionable education:** `View the Blueprint`
- **Avoid** vague CTA text that does not imply outcome or next step.

## Copy-Visual Pairing Guide
- Growth claim -> charts, metrics, upward arrows, count comparison.
- Spreadsheet pain claim -> real-world desk/screens workflow image.
- Product utility claim -> UI mocks with realistic interaction states.
- Support/emotional claim -> mascot/humanized visual metaphor.

## Creative Brief Copy Fields (Required)
- `primary_audience`
- `pain_statement`
- `outcome_claim`
- `proof_element`
- `format_target`
- `cta_text`
- `terminology_variant` (`vacation rental` or `holiday letting`)

## Copy QA Rubric (10-point)
- Clarity (0-2)
- Relevance to target persona (0-2)
- Specificity of benefit/proof (0-2)
- Visual-match readiness (0-2)
- CTA directness (0-2)

Passing threshold: `>=8`.
